## Ingredients

- 6 veal shanks
- Flour for dredging
- 2 carrots, diced
- 2 celery stalks, diced
- 1 onion, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 cup white wine
- 2 cups beef broth
- 28 oz can crushed tomatoes
- 2 bay leaves
- Fresh thyme
- Salt and pepper
- Gremolata: lemon zest, parsley, garlic

## Instructions

1. Season veal shanks, dredge in flour
2. Brown shanks in olive oil, set aside
3. Sauté carrots, celery, and onion until soft
4. Add garlic, cook 1 minute
5. Add wine, simmer until reduced
6. Add broth, tomatoes, bay leaves, and thyme
7. Return shanks to pot, bring to simmer
8. Cover and braise in 325°F oven for 2.5 hours
9. Top with gremolata before serving
10. Serve with risotto
