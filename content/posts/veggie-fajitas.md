## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp olive oil or more
- 2 bell peppers, sliced thin
- 1 large onion, sliced thin
- 10 ish mushrooms, sliced
- 2-3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 large lime
- 1 tbsp cumin
- 1 tbsp paprika
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp black pepper
- Generous pinch cayenne pepper
- Tortillas
- Sour cream
- Refried beans
- Guacamole or avocado
- Lettuce and tomato

## Instructions

1. In a large skillet, heat oil and add peppers, onion, mushrooms. Add in lime, cumin, paprika, salt, chili powder, black pepper, cayenne pepper and minced garlic. Cook until tender and add the veggie mix to the center of each tortilla. Top with the sour cream, beans, guac, lettuce and tomato
