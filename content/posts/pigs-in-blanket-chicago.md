## Ingredients

**Pigs n blanket basic:**
- One 8 oz tube crescent rolls
- 1 12 oz (all beef) package mini cocktail wieners (little smokies work)
- (optional) 4 tbsp unsalted butter to drizzle over top before oven

**Chicago variant:**
- Chopped dill pickles
- Toothpicks
- Chicago style relish
- Sport peppers
- Tomato, deseeded, diced
- Mustard
- Poppy seeds

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 375°F (can use toaster oven if easier). Take a tiny sliver of crescent roll and wrap around each mini weiner. Arrange on baking tray and (optional) drizzle a bit of butter and salt. 12-15 min
2. If just doing wieners, serve with spicy mustard dipping sauce
3. For chicago variant, have all ingredients ready and arrange to each hot dog using toothpick to spear ingredients. Then top with relish, mustard and poppy seeds. Enjoy
