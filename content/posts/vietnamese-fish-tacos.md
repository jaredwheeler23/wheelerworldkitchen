## Ingredients

**Pickled vegetables:**
- 1/2 cup rice vinegar
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 carrot, julienned
- 1/2 daikon radish, julienned
- 1/4 red onion, thinly sliced

**Fish:**
- 1.5 lbs white fish fillets (cod, tilapia, or mahi-mahi)
- 1/2 cup cornstarch
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- Vegetable oil for frying

**Sauce:**
- 1/4 cup mayonnaise
- 1 tbsp sriracha
- 1 tsp lime juice

**Assembly:**
- 8 small flour or corn tortillas
- Fresh cilantro leaves
- Fresh mint leaves
- Fresh basil leaves
- Lime wedges
- Sliced jalapeños (optional)

## Instructions

1. Make pickled vegetables: In a bowl, whisk together rice vinegar, sugar, and salt until dissolved. Add carrot, daikon, and red onion. Let sit for at least 20 minutes
2. Make sauce: Mix mayonnaise, sriracha, and lime juice in a small bowl. Set aside
3. Pat fish fillets dry with paper towels. Cut into strips about 1 inch wide
4. In a shallow dish, mix cornstarch, salt, and pepper. Dredge fish pieces in the mixture to coat lightly
5. Heat about 1/2 inch of oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. When oil is hot, fry fish pieces for 2-3 minutes per side until golden and crispy. Drain on paper towels
6. Warm tortillas in a dry skillet or over an open flame
7. Assemble tacos: Place fried fish on tortillas, top with pickled vegetables, drizzle with sauce, and add fresh herbs. Serve with lime wedges and jalapeños if desired

## Notes

- Can bake fish at 425°F for 15 minutes instead of frying for a lighter version
- Pickled vegetables can be made up to 3 days ahead and stored in the refrigerator
