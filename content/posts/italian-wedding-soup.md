# Italian Wedding Soup, The Dill Version

Traditional Italian wedding soup with a unique dill twist, featuring tender meatballs and greens.

## Ingredients

### Meatballs
- 2/3 pound ground chicken
- 2/3 pound italian sausage (mild)
- 1/2 cup bread crumbs, italian style works
- 2 large cloves garlic minced
- 3 tablespoons chopped cilantro, chopped small
- 1/2 cup parmesan cheese, the nice stuff NOT the powder
- 3 tablespoons milk or so
- 1 large egg
- Salt and pepper to taste (tbsp of each to start is good)

### Soup
- 2 tbsp good olive oil
- 1 yellow onion, minced
- 3 large carrots, chopped
- Cup chopped celery (or more doesn't hurt)
- 10 cups good chicken stock (kitchen basics)
- 1/2 cup dry white wine
- 1 cup small, star pasta
- 1/4 cup minced, fresh dill
- 12 ounces baby spinach, trimmed and lightly chopped

## Instructions

1. Combine all meatball ingredients in mixing bowl with fork and hands if needed. Scoop into greased sheet pan and spread out about 40 meatballs over pan. Note: these are small meatballs and a tsp could be used to scoop. Bake for 35 minutes at 350 degrees F.

2. Heat olive oil in large soup pot and saute carrots, onion, and celery until soft (6-8 min).

3. Add chicken stock and wine. Bring to boil.

4. Add pasta and cook 6-8 minutes.

5. Add dill, meatballs, and spinach. Cook another minute.

6. Check for salt and pepper.

7. Serve and sprinkle parmesan over bowls.
