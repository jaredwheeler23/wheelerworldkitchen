## Ingredients

**Meatballs:**
- 1/2 lb ground chicken
- 1/2 lb Italian sausage (mild)
- 1/2 cup bread crumbs, Italian style works
- 2 large cloves garlic, minced
- 3 tbsp chopped cilantro, chopped small
- 1/2 cup parmesan cheese, the nice stuff NOT the powder
- 3 tbsp milk or so
- 1 large egg
- Salt and pepper to taste (tbsp of each to start is good)

**Soup:**
- 2 tbsp good olive oil
- 1 yellow onion, minced
- 3 large carrots, chopped
- 1 cup chopped celery (or more doesn't hurt)
- 10 cups good chicken stock (kitchen basics)
- 1/2 cup dry white wine
- 1 cup small, star pasta
- 1/4 cup minced, fresh dill
- 12 oz baby spinach, trimmed and lightly chopped

## Instructions

1. Combine all meatball ingredients in mixing bowl with fork and hands if needed. Scoop into greased sheet pan and spread out ~40 meatballs over pan. Note: these are small meatballs and a tsp could be used to scoop. Bake for 35 minutes at 350°F
2. Heat olive oil in large soup pot and saute carrots, onion, and celery until soft (6-8 min)
3. Add chicken stock and wine. Bring to boil
4. Add pasta and cook 6-8 minutes
5. Add dill, meatballs, and spinach. Cook another minute
6. Check for salt and pepper
7. Serve and sprinkle parmesan over bowls
