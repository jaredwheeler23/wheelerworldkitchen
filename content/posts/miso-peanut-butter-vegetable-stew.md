## Ingredients

- 1 package of mushrooms, sliced
- 1 eggplant chunky cubes
- 2 bundles green onions
- 4 tbsp peanut butter (or almond butter)
- 1 tbsp miso paste
- 1 tsp maple sugar or similar
- 0.5 cup oat milk
- 5 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. In large, deep skillet, cook green onions in olive oil until soft. Add the eggplant and cook stirring occasionally until golden and cooked
2. Add in mushroom and cook another few minutes on lower heat
3. In separate bowl, mix together the rest of the ingredients and then add to the cooked mixture
4. It will start to thicken and bubble. Add a tbsp or so of water if becomes too thick
5. Serve over quinoa or rice
