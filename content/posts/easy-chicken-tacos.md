## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs or breasts
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 packet taco seasoning (or homemade: 2 tsp chili powder, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp paprika, 1/2 tsp garlic powder, 1/2 tsp onion powder, salt to taste)
- 1/2 cup water or chicken broth
- 8-10 small flour or corn tortillas

**Toppings:**
- 1 red onion, diced
- 1 bunch fresh cilantro, chopped
- 1 cup shredded cheese (cheddar or Mexican blend)
- 1 cup shredded lettuce
- 1 tomato, diced
- 1 avocado, sliced
- Sour cream
- Salsa
- Lime wedges

## Instructions

1. Cut chicken into bite-sized pieces or thin strips
2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat
3. Add chicken and cook for 5-7 minutes until browned and mostly cooked through
4. Add taco seasoning and water/broth. Stir well to coat chicken
5. Reduce heat to medium and simmer for 5-8 minutes until chicken is fully cooked and sauce has thickened
6. Warm tortillas in a dry skillet or microwave
7. Assemble tacos: place chicken in tortillas and top with desired toppings
8. Serve with lime wedges for squeezing

## Notes

- For extra flavor, marinate chicken in lime juice and spices for 30 minutes before cooking
- Can also cook chicken in slow cooker on low for 4-6 hours, then shred
- Leftover chicken makes great taco salads or burrito bowls
