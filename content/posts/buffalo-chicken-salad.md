## Ingredients

- 2 cups canned chicken or diced rotisserie
- ? cup buffalo sauce
- ? cup greek yogurt
- 2 tbsp mayo
- 1 tbsp ranch seasoning
- 1/2 cup celery diced 
- 1/2 cup red onion diced
- 1/4 cup green onion sliced
- 2 tbsp feta
- Salt and pepper to taste

- Cottage cheese


## Instructions

1. Mix together all but cottage cheese.  Mix that at serving.
2. Serve with bread for sandwiches or crackers for chips and dip.
3. Tastes absorb and mix better with time.  Refrigerate after mixing.
