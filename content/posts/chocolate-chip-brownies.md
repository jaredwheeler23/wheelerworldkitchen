## Ingredients

- 1 cup butter, melted
- 2 cups sugar
- 4 eggs
- 1 1/2 tsp vanilla
- 2/3 cup cocoa powder
- 1 cup flour
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1 1/2 cups chocolate chips

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F, grease 9x13 pan
2. Mix melted butter and sugar
3. Beat in eggs and vanilla
4. Combine cocoa, flour, salt, and baking powder
5. Stir dry ingredients into wet ingredients
6. Fold in chocolate chips
7. Pour into prepared pan
8. Bake 25-30 minutes (don't overbake for fudgy brownies)
9. Cool completely before cutting
