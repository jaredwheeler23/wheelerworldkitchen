# Thai Chicken Noodle Soup

Creamy Thai soup with green curry paste, coconut milk, and tender chicken.

## Ingredients

### Broth
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1/2 cup onion (or shallots) thinly sliced
- 1 tbsp ginger minced (about 1 inch knob)
- 2 tbsp Thai green curry paste
- 4 cups low sodium chicken broth (kitchen basics is best)
- 1 can coconut milk
- 2 tbsp fish sauce
- 4 packed tsp brown sugar
- 2 tbsp lime juice (1 lime)
- 1/2 tsp turmeric

### For Serving
- 4 oz thin rice noodles
- 2 cups shredded chicken (from a rotisserie chicken ideally)
- Handful fresh cilantro
- 3 bunches of green onions, only the white part sliced
- Sriracha
- Lime wedges

## Instructions

1. In a soup pot over medium low heat, cook onion and ginger. Stir frequently until softened. Add curry paste and cook, stirring constantly for about 1 minute more. Add chicken broth, coconut milk, fish sauce, brown sugar, lime juice, and turmeric. Bring to simmer and simmer for 5 minutes.

2. Meanwhile, cook the rice noodles according to package directions.

3. Serve. Divide chicken and noodles in bowls. Ladle in broth. Add as desired: green onions, cilantro, Sriracha and lime wedges.
