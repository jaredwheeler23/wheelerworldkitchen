## Ingredients

- 3 lbs chicken pieces
- 6 oz bacon, diced
- 8 oz mushrooms, quartered
- 12 pearl onions, peeled
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 carrots, sliced
- 2 cups red wine
- 1 cup chicken broth
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 3 tbsp flour
- 3 tbsp butter
- Fresh thyme and bay leaf
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Brown bacon in Dutch oven, set aside
2. Season chicken, brown in bacon fat, set aside
3. Sauté mushrooms and onions, set aside
4. Add carrots and garlic to pot, cook 2 minutes
5. Stir in flour, then add wine, broth, and tomato paste
6. Add thyme and bay leaf
7. Return chicken and bacon to pot
8. Simmer covered for 1.5 hours
9. Add mushrooms and onions, cook 15 more minutes
10. Serve with crusty bread or mashed potatoes
