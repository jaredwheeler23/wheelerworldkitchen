## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs flank steak, sliced thin
- 3 bell peppers, sliced
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 3 tbsp lime juice
- 2 tsp cumin
- 2 tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- Salt and pepper
- 8 flour tortillas
- Sour cream, guacamole, salsa

## Instructions

1. Marinate beef in lime juice and spices for 30 minutes
2. Heat oil in large skillet over high heat
3. Cook beef in batches until browned, set aside
4. Add peppers and onions, cook until tender-crisp
5. Return beef to skillet, toss to combine
6. Warm tortillas
7. Serve beef mixture in tortillas with desired toppings
