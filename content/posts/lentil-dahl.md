## Ingredients

- 2 cups red lentils
- 2 inch fresh ginger, grated
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1.5 tsp turmeric
- 2.5 tsp curry powder
- 5 cups water or some type of broth
- Salt and pepper to taste

**Optional add ons in dish (cook with lentils) or as toppings at end:**
- Butter
- Onion variety
- Jalapeno
- Cilantro
- Other veggies

## Instructions

1. Place all of primary ingredients in large saucepan and bring to simmer. Cook 15-20 minutes until everything is soft and it is a little thicker. Add optional items if cooking via the one-pot method
2. Add optional toppings
3. Serve with rice
