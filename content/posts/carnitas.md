## Ingredients

- 4 lbs pork shoulder, cut into chunks
- 1 onion, quartered
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 oranges, juiced
- 2 limes, juiced
- 2 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp oregano
- Salt and pepper
- Corn tortillas
- Fresh cilantro, diced onion, lime wedges

## Instructions

1. Place pork in slow cooker
2. Add onion, garlic, citrus juices, and spices
3. Cook on low for 6 hours until very tender
4. Remove pork and shred with forks
5. Broil shredded pork for 5-10 minutes until crispy edges form
6. Serve in warm corn tortillas with cilantro, onion, and lime
