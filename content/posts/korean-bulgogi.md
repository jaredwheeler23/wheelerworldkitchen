## Ingredients

- 2 lbs ribeye or sirloin, thinly sliced
- 1/2 cup soy sauce
- 1/4 cup brown sugar
- 2 tbsp sesame oil
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp ginger, grated
- 2 green onions, chopped
- 1 Asian pear, grated (or apple)
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds
- Black pepper

## Instructions

1. Mix all marinade ingredients (soy sauce through pear)
2. Add beef slices, massage marinade in
3. Refrigerate for at least 2 hours or overnight
4. Heat grill or skillet to high heat
5. Cook beef in batches, 2-3 minutes per side
6. Don't overcrowd the pan
7. Garnish with sesame seeds and green onions
8. Serve with rice and kimchi
