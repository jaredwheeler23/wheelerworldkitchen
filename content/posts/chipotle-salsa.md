## Ingredients

- 1 can (28 oz) whole peeled tomatoes, drained
- 2-4 chipotle peppers in adobo sauce (adjust to taste)
- 1 tbsp adobo sauce from the can
- 1/2 red onion, roughly chopped
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1/4 cup fresh cilantro
- Juice of 1 lime
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp salt, or more to taste
- 1/2 tsp black pepper

## Instructions

1. Add all ingredients to a food processor or blender
2. Pulse until desired consistency is reached. For chunky salsa, pulse briefly. For smooth salsa, blend longer
3. Taste and adjust seasonings. Add more chipotle peppers for extra heat, more lime for brightness, or more salt as needed
4. Let salsa sit for at least 15 minutes before serving to allow flavors to meld
5. Serve with tortilla chips, on tacos, or as a topping for grilled meats

## Notes

- Start with 2 chipotle peppers and add more if you want it spicier
- For a milder salsa, remove seeds from chipotle peppers
- Salsa will keep in refrigerator for up to 1 week
- Can roast fresh tomatoes instead of canned for a deeper flavor
- For a sweeter salsa, add 1 tsp honey or agave
