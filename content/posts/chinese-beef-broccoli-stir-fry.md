## Ingredients

- 1 lb flank steak sliced very thinly into bite size lengths
- Olive oil
- 1 lb broccoli cut into bite size florets
- 1 bell pepper cut into sliced bite size chunks
- 2 tsp sesame seeds (optional)

**Sauce:**
- 1 tsp ginger grated (or more)
- 4 cloves garlic minced
- 0.5 cup hot water
- 8 tbsp soy sauce
- 3 tbsp brown sugar
- 1.5 tbsp corn starch
- 1 tbsp chili garlic sauce (more or less to taste)
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 2 tbsp sesame oil

**Serving:**
- 1 cup uncooked rice (cooked according to package directions)

## Instructions

1. Combine all stir fry sauce ingredients in a bowl, stir well to dissolve the sugar, and set aside
2. Place broccoli in a large skillet over medium heat and add 1 Tbsp oil. Sauté 4-5 minutes, partially covered with lid, stirring or tossing several times. Remove from pan when done and set near
3. Increase heat to high heat and add 1 Tbsp oil. Add beef in a single layer and sauté ~2 minutes per side or just until cooked through
4. Add the sauce into beef, reduce heat to medium/low and simmer 3-4 minutes. It will thicken. Add broccoli and stir to combine. Stir in 1-2 Tbsp water to thin the sauce if desired. Simmer one more minute just enough to reheat the broccoli. Serve over white rice. Add sesame seeds (optional)
