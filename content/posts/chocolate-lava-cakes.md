## Ingredients

- 6 oz dark chocolate, chopped
- 1/2 cup butter
- 2 eggs
- 2 egg yolks
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 2 tbsp flour
- Pinch of salt
- Butter and cocoa for ramekins
- Vanilla ice cream for serving

## Instructions

1. Butter and cocoa dust 4 ramekins
2. Melt chocolate and butter together
3. Whisk eggs, egg yolks, and sugar until thick
4. Fold in melted chocolate
5. Gently fold in flour and salt
6. Divide among ramekins
7. Bake at 425°F for 12-14 minutes
8. Centers should be soft
9. Let stand 1 minute, then invert onto plates
10. Serve immediately with vanilla ice cream
