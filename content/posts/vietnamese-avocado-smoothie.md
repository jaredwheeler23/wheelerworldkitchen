## Ingredients

- 2 ripe avocados (peeled and without seed of course)
- 2.5 tbsp condensed milk (or more to taste)
- 0.5 cup whole milk (or almond milk)
- 0.25 cup ice

## Instructions
1. Put it all in a blender and puree.
2. Serve.
