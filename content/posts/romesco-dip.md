## Ingredients

- 16 oz jar roasted red peppers, drained
- 1/2 cup almonds, they can be plain or have some rosemary/mild seasonings
- 2 tbsp parsley
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic or more to taste
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper (or less to taste)

## Instructions

1. Throw it all in a food processor and blend to smooth. Adjust taste with salt and pepper as needed. Serve with crackers of some type or veggies
