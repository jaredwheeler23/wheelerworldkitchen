## Ingredients

- 2 cups heavy cream
- 1 vanilla bean (or 2 tsp vanilla extract)
- 5 egg yolks
- 1/2 cup sugar (plus extra for topping)
- Pinch of salt

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 325°F
2. Heat cream with vanilla bean (split and scraped) until simmering
3. Whisk egg yolks with sugar and salt
4. Slowly pour hot cream into egg mixture, whisking constantly
5. Strain through fine mesh sieve
6. Pour into 6 ramekins
7. Place ramekins in roasting pan, add hot water halfway up sides
8. Bake 40-45 minutes until set but still jiggly
9. Refrigerate at least 3 hours
10. Before serving, sprinkle sugar on top and caramelize with torch
