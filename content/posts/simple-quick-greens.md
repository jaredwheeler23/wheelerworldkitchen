## Details
- **Prep:** <5 min
- **Cook:** 5 min
- **Rating:** 10/10
- **Servings:** it is a light side but the below is 2-4

## Fresh Greens
- 2 bunches of kale (or 8 cups), cut into 1/2" or shorter sections
- 4-5 cloves garlic, minced
- Olive oil
- Few tbsps red pepper flakes, salt, black pepper

## Instructions Fresh Greens:
1. Heat oil and add garlic plus red pepper flakes.  Saute for a minute or so.  
2. Then add kale as much as you can fit.  Add more as it shrinks.  
3. Saute and add pepper/salt to taste until greens are limp and tender. 
4. Serve immediately as it does not stay warm long.
