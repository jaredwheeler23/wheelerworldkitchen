## Ingredients

- 2 lbs chicken breast, diced
- 2 cups cooked rice
- 1 can black beans, drained
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 6 large flour tortillas
- Cheese, sour cream, guacamole, salsa

## Instructions

1. Cook chicken in large skillet until golden
2. Add bell pepper and onion, cook until softened
3. Stir in rice, beans, cumin, and chili powder
4. Warm tortillas in microwave
5. Divide filling among tortillas
6. Add cheese and desired toppings
7. Fold in sides and roll up tightly
8. Optional: Grill seam-side down until golden
