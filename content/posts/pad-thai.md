## Ingredients

- 8 oz rice noodles
- 1 lb shrimp, peeled
- 3 eggs
- 3 tbsp tamarind paste
- 2 tbsp fish sauce
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- Bean sprouts
- Green onions, chopped
- Peanuts, crushed
- Lime wedges
- Vegetable oil

## Instructions

1. Soak rice noodles in warm water for 30 minutes, drain
2. Mix tamarind paste, fish sauce, and sugar for sauce
3. Heat oil in wok, scramble eggs and set aside
4. Stir-fry shrimp until pink, set aside
5. Add garlic to wok, then add noodles
6. Pour sauce over noodles, toss to coat
7. Add shrimp and eggs back to wok
8. Top with bean sprouts, green onions, and peanuts
9. Serve with lime wedges
