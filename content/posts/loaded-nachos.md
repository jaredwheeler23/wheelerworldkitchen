## Ingredients

- 1 bag tortilla chips
- 2 cups shredded cheddar or Mexican blend cheese
- 1 can black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 lb ground beef or turkey (optional)
- 1 packet taco seasoning
- 1 jalapeño, sliced
- 1/2 cup diced tomatoes
- 1/4 cup sliced black olives
- 1/4 cup diced red onion
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 1/2 cup guacamole
- 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
- Salsa for serving

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F
2. If using meat: In a skillet, cook ground beef/turkey until browned. Drain excess fat. Add taco seasoning and water according to package directions. Simmer until thickened
3. On a large baking sheet, spread tortilla chips in an even layer
4. Sprinkle half the cheese over the chips. Add black beans, cooked meat (if using), and half the jalapeños
5. Top with remaining cheese
6. Bake for 8-10 minutes until cheese is melted and bubbly
7. Remove from oven and immediately top with diced tomatoes, olives, red onion, remaining jalapeños, dollops of sour cream and guacamole, and fresh cilantro
8. Serve immediately with salsa on the side
