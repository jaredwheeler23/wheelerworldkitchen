## Ingredients

- 1 lb paneer cheese, cubed
- 1 lb fresh spinach
- 1 onion, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp ginger, minced
- 2 tomatoes, diced
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1/2 cup cream
- Salt
- Vegetable oil

## Instructions

1. Blanch spinach, blend into smooth paste
2. Fry paneer cubes until golden, set aside
3. Sauté cumin seeds, onion, garlic, and ginger
4. Add tomatoes and spices, cook until soft
5. Add spinach puree, simmer 10 minutes
6. Stir in cream
7. Add fried paneer
8. Serve with naan or rice
