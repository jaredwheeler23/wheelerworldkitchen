# Veggie Shepherds Pie

A hearty vegetarian version of shepherd's pie with lentils, vegetables, and kale-infused mashed potatoes.

## Ingredients

### Filling
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 large onion, diced small
- 3 large carrots, diced small
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1 cup brown or green lentils
- 4 tbsp red wine (or less of red wine vinegar)
- 1.5-2 cups water
- 1-2 tsp salt

### Mashed Potatoes
- 2-3 pounds yukon gold potatoes
- Few tsp salt
- 3 large garlic cloves, peeled but left whole
- Butter or olive oil (if vegetarian or making vegan)
- Black pepper
- 4 cups chopped kale (2 bunches)
- 2 cloves garlic, chopped roughly
- Parmesan cheese

## Instructions

### Filling
1. In small sauce pan on medium heat, saute onion, carrot and thyme until softened (about 5 minutes). Add lentils and wine and stir well, bring to lively simmer. Add 1.5 cups water and bring back to simmer. Lower heat, cover and cook until tender (about 40 minutes). Add more water if necessary. Taste at end and add salt if needed. Set aside.

### Mashed Potatoes
1. Get a large/wide soup pot and fill one layer (only one layer) densely with potatoes. Then add water until just the last millimeter of potato is above the water line. Add whole garlic, plenty of salt/pepper and simmer for about 30 minutes or until potatoes are well cooked (more time does not hurt).

2. Remove potatoes and garlic from water (DO NOT discard water or drain) and mash in large mixing bowl. Add butter (or olive oil if making vegan), salt and liquid from pot until you have a good consistency and flavor of mashed potatoes. Add black pepper to taste.

3. With final bit of garlic and chopped kale, heat more oil in frying pan and saute garlic briefly before adding kale (as much as you can fit). Add more kale as it shrinks until all is cooked. Stir constantly to avoid burning garlic.

### Assembly
1. Combine kale mixture with filling mixture and put in greased baking dish (pick a size so filling is only about 1/2 inch tall in dish and evenly spread). Add mashed potatoes and spread evenly on top with spoon (this layer can be thicker). Sprinkle heavily with parmesan cheese. Cook at 350 for 25 minutes. During final 2 minutes, set oven to broil and brown the cheesy top (watch carefully so no burning).

2. Remove from oven, slice and serve. It cools quickly enough.
