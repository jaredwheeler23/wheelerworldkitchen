## Ingredients

- 1 lb pasta (cooked to package directions)
- 4 tbsp butter
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1.5 cups heavy cream
- 0.5 cup milk (almond, oat or whole)
- 3 tsp salt
- 2 tsp lime juice or more to taste
- 8 bay leaves
- 1/2 cup queso fresco (or mozzarella works too)

## Instructions

1. In a sauce pan saute garlic in butter until fragrant
2. Add the rest of the ingredients to the bay leaves. Simmer for 5+ minutes. Stirring often. It should thicken a smidge. Remove bay leaves if you can
3. Add to the cooked pasta and stir well, add queso fresco too

## Notes

1. Serve with grilled shrimp or something tasty
2. The heavy cream, salt and lime juice is a combo to simulate Mexican cream. You can just use that instead if you got it
