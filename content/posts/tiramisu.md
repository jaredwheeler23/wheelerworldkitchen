## Ingredients

- 6 egg yolks
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 1 1/3 cups mascarpone cheese
- 2 cups heavy cream
- 2 cups strong espresso, cooled
- 3 tbsp coffee liqueur (optional)
- 40 ladyfinger cookies
- Cocoa powder for dusting
- Dark chocolate shavings (optional)

## Instructions

1. Whisk egg yolks and sugar until thick and pale
2. Add mascarpone, beat until smooth
3. Whip heavy cream to stiff peaks, fold into mascarpone mixture
4. Mix espresso with coffee liqueur
5. Quickly dip ladyfingers in espresso mixture
6. Layer dipped ladyfingers in 9x13 dish
7. Spread half the mascarpone cream over ladyfingers
8. Repeat with another layer
9. Dust generously with cocoa powder
10. Refrigerate at least 4 hours or overnight
