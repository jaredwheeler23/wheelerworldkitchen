## Ingredients

- 2 lbs ground lamb (or beef for Cottage Pie)
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 carrots, diced
- 1 cup peas
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 cup beef broth
- 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 4 cups mashed potatoes
- 2 tbsp butter
- Salt and pepper
- Fresh thyme

## Instructions

1. Brown ground lamb in large skillet, drain excess fat
2. Add onion and carrots, cook until softened
3. Stir in tomato paste, broth, Worcestershire, and thyme
4. Add peas, simmer 10 minutes
5. Transfer to baking dish
6. Spread mashed potatoes evenly over top
7. Dot with butter
8. Bake at 400°F for 25-30 minutes until golden
