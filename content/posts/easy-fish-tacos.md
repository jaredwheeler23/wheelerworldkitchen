## Ingredients

**Fish:**
- 5 white fish fillets (cod, tilapia, or mahi-mahi), thawed
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp garlic salt
- 1/4 tsp cayenne
- Lime juice

**Taco slaw:**
- 1 red onion, diced small
- 1 bunch cilantro, chopped
- 1/4 cup lime juice
- 1/2 cup red cabbage, shredded
- 1/4 cup chipotle mayo sauce

**Toppings (at the table):**
- Avocado slices
- Tomato, diced
- Salsa
- Cotija or other Mexican cheese
- Small flour or corn tortillas
- Extra chipotle mayo sauce

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven (or toaster oven) to 400°F
2. Place fish fillets on a lightly oiled baking sheet
3. Drizzle fish with olive oil and lime juice
4. Sprinkle all seasonings evenly over fish: cumin, chili powder, black pepper, garlic salt, and cayenne (go light on cumin and chili powder)
5. Bake for 10-15 minutes until fish flakes easily with a fork. Don't overcook
6. While fish cooks, mix taco slaw ingredients together in a bowl. Let sit to allow lime to "cook" the onion and mellow its bite
7. Warm tortillas in a dry skillet or microwave
8. Flake cooked fish into chunks. Assemble tacos with fish, slaw, and desired toppings
9. Serve with rice on the side if desired

## Notes

- For crispy fish, broil for last 2 minutes of cooking
- Make chipotle mayo by mixing mayo with chipotle peppers in adobo sauce to taste
- Can substitute fish with shrimp
