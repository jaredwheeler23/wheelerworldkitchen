## Ingredients

**Chicken:**
- 1.5 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 1/2 cup cornstarch
- 2 eggs, beaten
- Vegetable oil for frying
- Salt and pepper to taste

**Orange sauce:**
- 3/4 cup fresh orange juice
- Zest of 1 orange
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 1/3 cup rice vinegar
- 1/2 cup brown sugar
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes (or to taste)
- 2 tbsp cornstarch mixed with 2 tbsp water (slurry)

**Garnish:**
- Sesame seeds
- Sliced green onions
- Orange slices

## Instructions

1. Make the sauce: In a saucepan, combine orange juice, zest, soy sauce, vinegar, brown sugar, garlic, ginger, and red pepper flakes. Bring to a simmer over medium heat
2. Once simmering, add cornstarch slurry and stir until sauce thickens, about 2-3 minutes. Remove from heat and set aside
3. Season chicken pieces with salt and pepper
4. Dip chicken pieces in beaten egg, then dredge in cornstarch to coat well
5. Heat 1/2 inch of oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat
6. Fry chicken in batches for 3-4 minutes per side until golden brown and crispy. Drain on paper towels
7. In a large bowl or wok, toss fried chicken with orange sauce until well coated
8. Garnish with sesame seeds, green onions, and orange slices
9. Serve immediately over steamed white rice

## Notes

- For extra crispy chicken, double-coat by dipping in egg and cornstarch twice
- Can bake chicken at 425°F for 20 minutes instead of frying for a healthier version
- Add steamed broccoli to the dish for vegetables
