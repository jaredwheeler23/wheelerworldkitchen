## Ingredients

**Marinade:**
- 2 pounds shrimp (jumbo, peeled)
- 8 cloves garlic minced
- 4 tbsp chopped fresh parsley
- 2.5 tbsp tomato paste
- 2 tbsp fresh basil chopped
- 4 tsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper

**To brush on after cooking:**
- 3 cloves garlic minced
- 1/2 cup melted butter
- 2 tsp fresh parsley chopped
- Lemon wedges for serving

## Instructions

1. Using marinade ingredients and in a bowl or bag, marinate for 2 hours
2. Put shrimp on skewers and grill until done (4 min a side is a good starting point or until pink all around, DO NOT overcook)
3. Place on platter and brush on the final set of ingredients. Serve with lemon wedges
