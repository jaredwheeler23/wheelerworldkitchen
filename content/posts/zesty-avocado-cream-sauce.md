## Ingredients

- 1 ripe avocado
- 1 cup cilantro leaves (loose, not packed)
- 1/3 cup sour cream
- 1 jalapeno (seeded)
- 2 garlic cloves
- 2 tbsp lime juice
- 1 tsp salt

## Instructions

1. Food process all ingredients together
2. Add to your favorite burritos, tacos, etc. Goes well when making chipotle bowls or burritos
