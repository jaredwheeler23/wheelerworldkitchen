## Ingredients

- 2 lbs chicken breasts (or thighs), cut into 1 inch cubes
- 1 lemon for juice
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 3 bunches green onion, white part only, sliced
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 2 large bell peppers, diced
- 3 large cloves garlic, minced
- 1.5 inch piece ginger, minced
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- 2 cups chopped garden tomato
- 1 tsp thyme (fresh or dried)
- 0.5-1 tsp red pepper flakes
- 0.25-0.5 jar peanut butter (see notes)
- 1.25 cups water or broth
- 1.5 cups jasmine rice

## Instructions

1. In bowl, toss chicken with lemon juice and salt. Let sit for 10 minutes
2. In large pot over medium heat, cook chicken until white on outside. Remove and set aside
3. Add more oil to pot, cook green onions, onion, and bell peppers until tender
4. Add garlic, ginger and chili powder. Cook, stirring constantly for 1-2 minutes
5. Add tomatoes, thyme, red pepper flakes, peanut butter, partly cooked chicken, and water. Stir well until peanut butter is evenly mixed
6. Bring to a simmer and reduce to low heat. Simmer for 30-40 minutes (be careful not to burn the peanut butter)
7. Meanwhile, cook rice according to package directions
8. Serve stew over rice. Add salt to taste

## Notes

- For peanut allergies, sunflower or almond spread works just as well. Just add a touch of raw sugar and salt to adjust the flavors
- I use 0.25 jar of peanut butter for a thicker texture. For more liquid, use less
