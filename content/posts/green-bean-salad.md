# Green Bean Salad

A refreshing salad featuring crisp green beans, feta cheese, and a simple oregano vinaigrette.

## Ingredients

### Salad
- 1 lb or 4 cups fresh green beans (stem on)
- 1/2 head romaine lettuce (2 cups)
- 1 red onion sliced thin
- 1 cup feta crumbles
- 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes halved
- 1/4 cup chopped walnuts

### Dressing
- 1 tbsp fresh oregano chopped
- 2 tsp red wine vinegar
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp olive oil

## Instructions

1. Cook beans (stem on) until crisp. Leave stems on but cut off thin, wooden parts as desired.

2. Mix ingredients and dressing separate. Then combine in a large serving bowl!
