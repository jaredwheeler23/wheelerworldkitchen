## Ingredients

Ingredients (marinade)
- 1-2 pounds of steak, dealers choice
- 6 minced garlic cloves
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1/2 tsp cayenne
- 1/2 cup soy sauce
- 1/4 cup worcester sauce
- 2 tbsp oil (any kind)
- ? cup lime juice
- 2 tsp salt or more to taste
- 2 tsp black pepper


## Instructions

1. Place all ingredients in large bag or tupperware.  Shake well and sit in fridge for 2 hours minimum.  Shake the ingredients around every once in a while.  Let warm up on counter for 0.5-1 hour before grilling.
