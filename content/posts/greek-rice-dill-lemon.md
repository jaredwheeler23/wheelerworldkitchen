## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 small onion diced small
- 3 cloves garlic minced
- 1.5 cups uncooked white rice
- 3 cups chicken broth
- 1 tbsp fresh dill (or less dried)
- 1-2 tbsp lemon juice

## Instructions

1. Saute over med heat onion and garlic until translucent
2. Add rice to pan and saute for 5 minutes stirring frequently
3. Either transfer to a pot to cook rice in or move this all to a rice cooker. Regardless, pour in chicken broth, mix, and simmer until rice is cooked
4. Add in dill, lemon juice. Mix and serve
