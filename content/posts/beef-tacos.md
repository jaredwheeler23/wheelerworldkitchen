## Ingredients

- 2 lbs ground beef
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp paprika
- Salt and pepper
- 12 taco shells
- Toppings: lettuce, tomatoes, cheese, sour cream, salsa

## Instructions

1. Brown ground beef in large skillet over medium-high heat
2. Add onion and garlic, cook until softened
3. Drain excess fat
4. Add spices and 1/2 cup water
5. Simmer for 10 minutes until mixture thickens
6. Warm taco shells according to package directions
7. Fill shells with beef and desired toppings
