## Ingredients

- 4 ripe avocados
- 1 lime, juiced
- 1/2 red onion, finely diced
- 1 jalapeño, seeded and minced
- 2 Roma tomatoes, diced
- 1/4 cup fresh cilantro, chopped
- Salt and pepper
- Tortilla chips for serving

## Instructions

1. Cut avocados in half, remove pit
2. Scoop flesh into bowl and mash with fork
3. Add lime juice immediately to prevent browning
4. Fold in onion, jalapeño, tomatoes, and cilantro
5. Season with salt and pepper to taste
6. Serve immediately with tortilla chips
