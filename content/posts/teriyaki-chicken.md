## Ingredients

**Chicken:**
- 1.5 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Sesame seeds for garnish
- Sliced green onions for garnish

**Teriyaki sauce:**
- 1/2 cup soy sauce
- 1/4 cup water
- 1/4 cup mirin (or substitute with 2 tbsp rice vinegar + 2 tbsp water)
- 3 tbsp brown sugar
- 2 tbsp honey
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1 tbsp cornstarch mixed with 1 tbsp water (slurry)

## Instructions

1. Make the teriyaki sauce: In a small saucepan, combine soy sauce, water, mirin, brown sugar, honey, garlic, and ginger. Bring to a simmer over medium heat
2. Once simmering, add cornstarch slurry and stir until sauce thickens, about 2 minutes. Remove from heat and set aside
3. Season chicken pieces with salt and pepper
4. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat
5. Add chicken and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until browned and cooked through
6. Pour teriyaki sauce over chicken and toss to coat well. Cook for another 2-3 minutes to let sauce caramelize slightly
7. Remove from heat and garnish with sesame seeds and sliced green onions
8. Serve over steamed white rice with steamed broccoli or other vegetables on the side

## Notes

- For extra caramelization, increase heat at the end and let sauce bubble and thicken
- Can grill or bake chicken instead of pan-frying
- Add pineapple chunks for a Hawaiian twist
- Sauce can be made ahead and stored in refrigerator for up to 1 week
