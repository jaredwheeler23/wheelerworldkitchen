## Ingredients

- 1.25 lbs roma tomatoes (5-6)
- 1 (14.5 oz) can petite diced tomatoes (low salt and maybe fire roasted)
- 3 green onions, ends trimmed
- 1/4 red onion
- 1 jalapeño (up to you on seeded, usually not)
- Batch cilantro
- 1 large clove garlic
- 2 tbsp lime juice
- 1/2 tsp chili powder
- 1/4 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp salt or little less
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Combine all ingredients in a food processor (in batches if necessary) and combine. If you have time, allow salsa to refrigerate for a few hours before serving to better blend spices (it is worth it)
