## Ingredients

- 1 lb penne pasta
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp red pepper flakes (adjust to taste)
- 28 oz can crushed tomatoes
- 1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped
- Salt
- Parmesan cheese for serving

## Instructions

1. Cook penne in salted boiling water until al dente
2. Heat olive oil in large skillet
3. Sauté garlic and red pepper flakes until fragrant
4. Add crushed tomatoes and salt
5. Simmer for 15 minutes
6. Toss cooked pasta with sauce
7. Garnish with parsley and Parmesan
