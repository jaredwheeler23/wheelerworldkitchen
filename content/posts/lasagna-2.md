## Ingredients

- 12 lasagna noodles
- 1 lb ground turkey (or beef)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 8 oz mushrooms, sliced
- 1 jar (24 oz) marinara sauce
- 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes
- 2 cups cottage cheese
- 1 egg
- 2 cups shredded mozzarella
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan
- 2 cups fresh spinach, chopped
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 375°F
2. Cook lasagna noodles according to package directions. Drain and set aside
3. In a large skillet, heat olive oil over medium heat. Add ground turkey and cook until browned. Add onion, garlic, bell pepper, and mushrooms. Cook until vegetables are tender
4. Stir in marinara sauce and diced tomatoes. Simmer for 10 minutes. Season with salt, pepper, and Italian seasoning
5. In a bowl, mix cottage cheese, egg, and half of the Parmesan cheese
6. In a 9x13 inch baking dish, spread a thin layer of meat sauce. Layer 4 noodles, half of the cottage cheese mixture, half of the spinach, half of the remaining meat sauce, and 2/3 cup mozzarella. Repeat layers. Top with remaining 4 noodles, sauce, mozzarella, and Parmesan
7. Cover with foil and bake for 30 minutes. Remove foil and bake for an additional 15 minutes until cheese is bubbly and golden
8. Let stand 10 minutes before serving
