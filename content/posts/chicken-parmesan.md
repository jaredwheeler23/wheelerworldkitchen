## Ingredients

- 4 chicken breasts, pounded thin
- 2 cups breadcrumbs
- 1 cup Parmesan cheese, grated
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 1 cup flour
- 2 cups marinara sauce
- 2 cups mozzarella cheese, shredded
- Fresh basil
- Salt and pepper
- Olive oil for frying

## Instructions

1. Set up breading station: flour, beaten eggs, breadcrumbs mixed with Parmesan
2. Season chicken with salt and pepper
3. Dredge in flour, dip in egg, coat with breadcrumb mixture
4. Fry in olive oil until golden on both sides
5. Place chicken in baking dish, top with marinara and mozzarella
6. Bake at 400°F for 15-20 minutes until cheese is melted
7. Garnish with fresh basil
8. Serve over pasta
