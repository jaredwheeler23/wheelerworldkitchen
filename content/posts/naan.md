## Details
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 10 min
- **Rating:** 10/10
- **Servings:** 3-4

## Ingredients
- 1.5 cups all purpose flour
- 1 cup plain yogurt (greek is fine)
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp salt

## Directions
1. Mix ingredients together in a bowl well and kneed for a minute or so to a ball.  If way too sticky add flour, if way too dry to come together in a ball, add a small amount of yogurt.
2. Use a rolling pin with flour to make 6 super thin (~6 in) diameter pies
3. Heat large flying pan (NO oil) up super hot and cook one at a time.  A minute or so each side (time depends on how hot).  If you get it really thin it will bubble as Naan typically does.
