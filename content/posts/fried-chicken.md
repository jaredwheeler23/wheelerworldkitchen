## Ingredients

- 3 lbs chicken pieces
- 2 cups buttermilk
- 2 cups flour
- 2 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp cayenne pepper
- Salt and pepper
- Vegetable oil for frying

## Instructions

1. Marinate chicken in buttermilk for at least 2 hours
2. Mix flour and all spices in large bowl
3. Heat oil to 350°F in deep fryer or large pot
4. Dredge chicken pieces in seasoned flour
5. Fry in batches for 12-15 minutes until golden
6. Drain on paper towels
7. Season with additional salt while hot
