## Ingredients

- 2 lbs ground beef
- 1 cup breadcrumbs
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 1/2 cup milk
- 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- Salt and pepper

**For glaze:**
- 1/2 cup ketchup
- 2 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp mustard

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F
2. Mix all meatloaf ingredients in large bowl
3. Shape into loaf and place in baking dish
4. Mix glaze ingredients and brush over meatloaf
5. Bake for 1 hour until internal temp reaches 160°F
6. Let rest 10 minutes before slicing
