## Ingredients

- 1 lb elbow macaroni
- 4 tbsp butter
- 4 tbsp flour
- 3 cups milk
- 3 cups shredded cheddar cheese
- 1 cup shredded mozzarella
- Salt, pepper, and paprika
- 1 cup breadcrumbs
- 2 tbsp melted butter

## Instructions

1. Cook macaroni according to package directions, drain
2. In large pot, melt butter and whisk in flour
3. Gradually add milk, whisking constantly
4. Cook until thickened, about 5 minutes
5. Remove from heat, stir in cheeses until melted
6. Add cooked macaroni, season with salt, pepper, and paprika
7. Transfer to greased baking dish
8. Mix breadcrumbs with melted butter, sprinkle on top
9. Bake at 375°F for 25-30 minutes until golden
