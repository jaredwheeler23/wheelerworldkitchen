## Ingredients

- 2 lbs chicken breast, cubed
- 1 cup flour
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 1 bell pepper, cubed
- 1 onion, cubed
- 1 cup pineapple chunks
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 1/2 cup ketchup
- 1/3 cup vinegar
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- Vegetable oil

## Instructions

1. Mix sauce ingredients (sugar through cornstarch)
2. Dip chicken in egg, then flour
3. Deep fry chicken until golden and crispy
4. In separate pan, stir-fry peppers and onions
5. Add pineapple and sauce, simmer until thickened
6. Add fried chicken, toss to coat
7. Serve over rice
