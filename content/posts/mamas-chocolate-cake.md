# Mama's Chocolate Cake

A moist, rich chocolate layer cake with homemade chocolate frosting.

## Ingredients

### Cake
- 2 cup sugar white
- 1.75 all purpose flour
- 0.75 cup cocoa powder
- 1.5 tsp baking powder
- 1.5 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 eggs
- 1 cup milk (whole preferred)
- 0.5 cup vegetable oil
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 cup boiling water

### Frosting
- 0.5 cup butter
- 0.67 cup cocoa
- 3 cup powdered sugar
- 0.33 cup whole milk
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350 F and grease two 9" round baking pans.

2. Combine dry ingredients in large bowl. Add eggs, milk, oil and vanilla. Beat on med speed 2 minutes. Stir in boiling water (this makes it thin). Pour into pans and bake 30-35 minutes. Cool completely before adding frosting.

3. Frosting: Melt butter and stir in cocoa. Add powdered sugar and milk, beating on med speed. Add more milk if needed. Stir in vanilla. Makes about 2 cups total.
