## Ingredients

**For dough:**
- 3 cups flour
- 1 packet active dry yeast
- 1 cup warm water
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp salt

**For topping:**
- 1 cup crushed tomatoes
- 8 oz fresh mozzarella, sliced
- Fresh basil leaves
- Olive oil
- Salt

## Instructions

1. Mix yeast with warm water and sugar, let sit 5 minutes
2. Combine flour and salt, add yeast mixture and olive oil
3. Knead until smooth, let rise 1.5 hours
4. Divide dough in half, roll into circles
5. Spread crushed tomatoes on dough
6. Top with mozzarella slices
7. Drizzle with olive oil, sprinkle with salt
8. Bake at 475°F for 12-15 minutes until crust is golden
9. Top with fresh basil after baking
