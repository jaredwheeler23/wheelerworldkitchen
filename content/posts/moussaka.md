## Details
- **Prep:** 45 min
- **Cook:** 1.25 hours
- **Rating:** 10/10
- **Servings:** 4-6

## Ingredients
- 2 lb eggplant sliced 0.3" thickness (discard ends)
- Salt, 2-3 tbsp olive oil

- 1-2 tbsp Olive oil
- 1 yellow onion diced
- 4-5 garlic cloves minced
- 1.5 lb ground beef or lamb
- 1/2 cup red wine (or a few tbsp red wine vinegar)
- 1 14 oz can crushed tomatoes mildly drained
- 3 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 cup beef stock/broth 
- Spices: 1 beef bouillon cube (or 1 tsp powder), 2 bay leaves, 2 tsp dried oregano, 1.5 tsp any sugar, 1/2 tsp cinnamon, 1 tsp salt or more 

- 4 tbsp butter
- 5 tbsp plain flour
- 2.5 cups milk (any kind or make)
- 1/4 tsp nutmeg
- ½ cup parmesan cheese (the coarse shredded, NOT the powder)
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp chicken stock powder or similar (salt can be substituted)
- 1 tsp black pepper

- Either ½ cup parmesan OR breadcrumbs (for topping before baking to give it a crunch)

## Directions
1. Place eggplant on large baking sheet, sprinkle with salt, sweat for >30 minutes.  Then wipe clean really well to get rid of moisture AND salt.  Then bake at 450 F for 15-20 minutes until slightly browned.
2. Meanwhile, in large, deep skillet, cook garlic, meat until it browns.  Add wine and cook for a couple minutes until alcohol is cooked out.  Add remining ingredients up to cinnamon/salt and simmer for 15 minutes or until it is a reasonably thick sauce.
3. Meanwhile again, make the sauce.  Melt the butter in a medium sized pan, add flour and cook for 1 minute stirring constantly.  Then add milk slowly stirring all the while.  It will thicken in a few minutes and continue to stir until it does.  (Use a whisk).  After thickened, remove from heat and add rest of ingredients except the toppings.  Stir well.
4. Assemble in a large, deep baking dish.  1 layer eggplant on bottom -> all of meat sauce -> another layer of eggplant (this should use all of it) -> all of milk/butter sauce.  Top with optional parmesan/breadcrumbs.
5. Bake at 350 F for 30-40 minutes uncovered until golden brown.  Allow at least a few minutes to cool and then serve.
