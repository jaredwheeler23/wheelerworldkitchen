## Details
- **Prep:** 20 min
- **Cook:** 20 min
- **Rating:** 10/10
- **Servings:** 3-4

## Ingredients (meat)
- 2 lb ground turkey
- 2 tbsp mayonnaise
- 2 tbsp dijon mustard
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tsp onion powder
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 cup chopped parsley (1 bunch)
- Few tbsp olive oil

## Toppings
- Sourdoubh sliced bread
- Sliced tomatoes
- Mayonnaise
- Avocado spread
- Lettuce
- Red onion sliced thin
- Plain mustard

## Instructions:
1. Mix all meat together well using hands and make patties (should make ~7).  Grill until cooked through.
2. Serve over toasted sourdough bread with toppings above as suggested.
