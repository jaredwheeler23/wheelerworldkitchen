## Ingredients

- 3 lbs pork shoulder, cubed
- 2 lbs tomatillos, husked and halved
- 2 poblano peppers, roasted and peeled
- 2 jalapeños
- 1 onion, quartered
- 4 cloves garlic
- 1 cup chicken broth
- 1 tsp cumin
- Fresh cilantro
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Roast tomatillos and peppers under broiler until charred
2. Blend roasted vegetables with onion, garlic, and broth
3. Brown pork in batches in Dutch oven
4. Add blended sauce and cumin
5. Simmer covered for 4 hours until pork is tender
6. Season with salt and pepper
7. Garnish with fresh cilantro
8. Serve with rice or in tortillas
