## Ingredients

**Crust:**
- 2 1/2 cups flour
- 1 cup cold butter, cubed
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 6-8 tbsp ice water

**Filling:**
- 6-8 apples, peeled and sliced
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 2 tbsp flour
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp nutmeg
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 egg for egg wash

## Instructions

1. Make crust: Mix flour, salt, sugar. Cut in butter until crumbly
2. Add ice water until dough forms. Divide in half, chill 30 minutes
3. Mix apples with sugar, flour, and spices
4. Roll out half the dough, place in 9-inch pie pan
5. Add apple filling, dot with butter
6. Roll out remaining dough for top crust
7. Seal edges, cut vents in top
8. Brush with egg wash, sprinkle with sugar
9. Bake at 425°F for 15 minutes, then 350°F for 45 minutes
10. Cool before slicing
