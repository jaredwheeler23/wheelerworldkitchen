## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs flank steak, sliced thin
- 1 lb broccoli florets
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp ginger, minced
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 2 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 1/2 cup beef broth
- Sesame oil
- Vegetable oil

## Instructions

1. Mix soy sauce, oyster sauce, cornstarch, sugar, and broth
2. Marinate beef in half the sauce for 15 minutes
3. Blanch broccoli in boiling water for 2 minutes, drain
4. Heat wok with oil over high heat
5. Stir-fry beef until browned, set aside
6. Add garlic and ginger, cook 30 seconds
7. Add broccoli and remaining sauce
8. Return beef to wok, toss until sauce thickens
9. Drizzle with sesame oil
10. Serve over rice
