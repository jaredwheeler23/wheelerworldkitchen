## Ingredients

- 1.5 cups Arborio rice
- 8 oz mushrooms, sliced
- 6 cups chicken or vegetable broth, warmed
- 1 onion, finely diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 cup white wine
- 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese
- 3 tbsp butter
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Fresh parsley
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Sauté mushrooms in butter until golden, set aside
2. In same pot, heat olive oil and cook onion until soft
3. Add garlic and rice, stir for 2 minutes
4. Add wine and stir until absorbed
5. Add broth one ladle at a time, stirring constantly
6. Continue adding broth until rice is creamy and al dente (about 25 minutes)
7. Stir in mushrooms, Parmesan, and remaining butter
8. Season with salt and pepper, garnish with parsley
