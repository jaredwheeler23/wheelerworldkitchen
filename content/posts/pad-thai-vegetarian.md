## Ingredients

**Noodles:**
- 8-12 oz flat rice noodles

**Vegetables:**
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 large head broccoli, cut into 1 inch pieces
- 12 oz whole mushrooms
- 1 bell pepper, thinly sliced
- 1 yellow onion, sliced thin
- 3 bunches green onions, diced (white and light green parts)
- 3 eggs

**Sauce:**
- 4 tbsp fish sauce
- 1.5 tbsp soy sauce
- 6.5 tbsp brown sugar
- 2.5 tbsp rice vinegar
- 2 tbsp sriracha hot sauce (or less to taste)
- 5 tbsp almond butter or peanut butter

**Mix-ins after cooking:**
- 3/4 cup roasted peanuts or almonds, chopped
- 1 cup bean sprouts
- Juice of 2 limes
- 1 bunch cilantro, chopped roughly
- Additional green onions (optional)

## Instructions

1. Cook noodles according to package directions. Drain and set aside. If they get sticky, rinse with cold water right before serving, then drain again
2. Mix all sauce ingredients together in a bowl. Set aside
3. Heat oil in large wok or deep pan over medium-high heat
4. Add broccoli and garlic, cook for 2 minutes
5. Add remaining vegetables (mushrooms, bell pepper, yellow onion, green onions). Cover and cook, stirring occasionally, until tender
6. Push vegetables to side of wok. Crack eggs onto empty side and scramble, chopping into small bits with spatula
7. Mix everything together. Add noodles, sauce, and all mix-ins (peanuts/almonds, bean sprouts, lime juice, cilantro)
8. Toss well to combine and heat through
9. Serve with additional green onions, peanuts/almonds, cilantro, and lime wedges on the side

## Notes

- For protein, swap broccoli and mushrooms for chicken or shrimp
- Can use peanut butter instead of almond butter if no allergies
- Adjust spice level with more or less sriracha
