## Ingredients

- 20 oz dark chocolate, chopped into small pieces (or chocolate chips)
- 1.75 cups heavy cream (more or less for desired consistency)
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

**Dipping ideas:**
- Strawberries (halved or whole)
- Blueberries
- Blackberries
- Banana slices
- Apple slices
- Pear slices
- Pound cake cubes
- Rice crispy treats, cut into bite-sized pieces
- Marshmallows
- Pretzels
- Cookies

## Instructions

1. Place chocolate pieces in a fondue pot or heatproof bowl
2. In a small saucepan, heat heavy cream over medium heat until it just begins to simmer (don't let it boil)
3. Pour hot cream over chocolate and let sit for 1 minute
4. Stir gently until chocolate is completely melted and mixture is smooth
5. Stir in vanilla extract
6. If using fondue pot, transfer chocolate to pot and keep warm over low heat. If using a bowl, you can place it over a pot of barely simmering water to keep warm
7. Arrange dipping items on a platter
8. Serve with fondue forks or skewers for dipping

## Notes

- For a thicker fondue, use less cream. For a thinner, more pourable fondue, use more cream
- Can add 1-2 tbsp liqueur (Grand Marnier, Kahlua, or Bailey's) for adult version
- Stir frequently to prevent chocolate from burning or forming a skin
- If chocolate gets too thick, whisk in a splash of warm cream
- For milk chocolate fondue, use milk chocolate chips instead of dark
