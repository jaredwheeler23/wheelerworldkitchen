# Lasagna (Meat or Veggie)

Traditional Italian lasagna with rich marinara sauce, three cheeses, and optional Italian sausage.

## Ingredients

### Sauce
- 28 oz jar of good marinara sauce
- 2 large garden tomatoes, chopped
- 2 cloves of garlic, chopped
- Cup spinach, chopped
- 1 lb ground hot Italian sausage (optional and leave out for vegetarian option)
- 1.5 cups water

### Cheese Mixture
- 12 oz ricotta cheese
- 3 cups mozzarella
- 1/2 cup parmesan cheese
- 2 eggs
- Small bunch parsley

### Assembly
- 9 uncooked lasagna noodles
- 1 garden tomato thinly sliced
- 1/2 cup mozzarella

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees F.

2. In deep, fry pan cook sausage and garlic until cooked through. Add and cook spinach for another minute. Add marinara sauce, tomatoes, and 1.5 cups water. Simmer for 10 minutes.

3. In large mixing bowl, mix: ricotta, mozzarella, parmesan cheese, eggs, and parsley.

4. In deep baking dish, layer in order: some sauce → 3 noodles → half cheese mixture → some sauce → 3 noodles → other half cheese mixture → some sauce → 3 noodles → any remaining sauce → thinly sliced tomato evenly on top → mozzarella sprinkled on top

5. Cover with tinfoil and bake for 1 hour. Last 15 minutes, remove the tinfoil.
