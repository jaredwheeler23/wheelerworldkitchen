## Ingredients

- 4 lbs pork shoulder
- 2 tbsp brown sugar
- 2 tbsp paprika
- 1 tbsp garlic powder
- 1 tbsp onion powder
- 1 tsp cayenne pepper
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 cups BBQ sauce
- Hamburger buns

## Instructions

1. Mix brown sugar and spices to create a dry rub
2. Rub spice mixture all over the pork shoulder
3. Place pork in slow cooker
4. Cook on low for 8 hours until tender
5. Shred meat with two forks
6. Mix in BBQ sauce
7. Serve on toasted buns
