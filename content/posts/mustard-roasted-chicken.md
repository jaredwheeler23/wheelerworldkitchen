## Ingredients

- 2-3 pounds chicken thighs (flexible on volume, how hungry are ya)
- 1/4 cup or more dijon mustard
- 2-3 tbsp honey OR maple syrup
- 1/4 cup water for sauce
- 1 tsp salt or more to taste
- 1/2 cup tarragon leaves (or basil if that is easier to find)
- Additional (optional) seasonings: garlic salt, pepper

## Instructions

1. Put chicken in deep dish pan and preheat oven to 430°F
2. Mix rest of ingredients together well in small dish
3. Spread out on chicken in pan, add water so there is at least a half inch of water in pan (you do not want the chicken to dry out)
4. Cover with tinfoil and bake for at minimum of an hour. Cooking it more will make it more tender and flaky which if there is time, reduce heat to 300°F at hour mark and cook another 1-2 hours
5. Check periodically to make sure water has not evaporated and add more if needed
6. Serve with veggies and a starch!
