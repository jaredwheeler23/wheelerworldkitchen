## Ingredients

- 50-60 frozen meatballs (homestyle seasoning variety work best, don't buy italian or other flavors)
- one 20 oz can pineapple chunks (not crushed, not rings, etc.)
- 2.5 cups decent BBQ sauce, baby rays as an example

## Instructions

1. Put it all in a pot, bring to simmer, stir well, and let simmer for 20 minutes

## Notes

1. The frozen meatballs are necessary and don't bother making nice ones, the sauce and pineapple influence the flavor so much, it doesn't make a difference
2. Serve over rice to make a main course
3. Spear a meatball and pineapple chunk with toothpicks to make appetizers
