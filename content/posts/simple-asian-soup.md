## Ingredients

- 4 cups broth (chicken or veggie or beef, whatever you got)
- 4-6 oz noodles (your choice on rice noodles, ramen, or other)
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- about a handful of greens chopped small, options: green onions, leaves of bok choy, finely diced celery, etc.

**Optional Toppings:**
- can add leftover cooked meats or hardboiled egg as well

## Instructions

1. Bring broth to simmer and add rest of ingredients.  Simmer until the noodles are done as per package directions.
2. Serve in large bowls with soup spoons.
