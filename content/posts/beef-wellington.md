## Ingredients

- 2 lbs beef tenderloin
- 1 lb mushrooms, finely chopped
- 2 shallots, minced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 8 oz pâté or liver mousse
- 8 slices prosciutto
- 1 sheet puff pastry
- 2 egg yolks, beaten
- Fresh thyme
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Sear beef on all sides, season with salt and pepper, cool
2. Sauté mushrooms, shallots, and garlic until dry (duxelles)
3. Season duxelles with thyme, salt, and pepper
4. Lay out prosciutto on plastic wrap, overlapping
5. Spread pâté over prosciutto
6. Spread duxelles over pâté
7. Place beef on top, roll tightly in plastic, chill 30 minutes
8. Roll out puff pastry, wrap around beef
9. Brush with egg wash
10. Bake at 425°F for 35-40 minutes until pastry is golden
11. Rest 10 minutes before slicing
