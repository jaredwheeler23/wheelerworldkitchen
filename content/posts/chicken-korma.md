## Ingredients

- 2 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil or ghee
- 1 large onion, finely diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1 cup plain yogurt (full fat preferred)
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 1/2 cup chicken broth
- 2 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper (or to taste)
- 1 tsp salt or more to taste
- 1/4 cup sliced almonds (optional, for garnish)
- Fresh cilantro, chopped

## Instructions

1. Heat oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Add chicken pieces and brown on all sides, about 5 minutes. Remove and set aside
2. In the same pot, add onion and cook until golden and softened, about 8 minutes
3. Add garlic and ginger, cook for 1 minute until fragrant
4. Add all spices (garam masala, coriander, cumin, turmeric, cayenne, salt) and stir for 30 seconds
5. Return chicken to pot. Add yogurt, cream, and chicken broth. Stir well to combine
6. Bring to a simmer, then reduce heat to low. Cover and simmer for 20-25 minutes until chicken is cooked through and sauce has thickened
7. Taste and adjust salt and spices as needed
8. Garnish with sliced almonds and fresh cilantro
9. Serve over basmati rice with naan bread on the side

## Notes

- For a richer korma, add 2 tbsp ground cashews or almond butter to the sauce
- Can substitute chicken with lamb, beef, or vegetables
