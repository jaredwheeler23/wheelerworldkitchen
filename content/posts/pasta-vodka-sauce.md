## Ingredients

**Sauce:**
- 6 tbsp butter
- 1 huge onion (or two med) diced
- 7-8 cloves of garlic minced
- Tbsp red pepper flakes
- Salt to taste
- 1-2 6-oz can tomato paste (only one is required)
- Two 14-oz can whole peeled tomatoes
- 2 cups heavy cream
- 1/2 cup vodka or more to taste
- 5 oz parmesan cheese (or fancier parmesan if you got it)

**Main:**
- 1 lb pasta, dealers choice
- 2 lb meatballs

## Instructions

1. In a large (3- or 4-quart) saucepan or small Dutch oven, melt butter over medium heat. Add onion, garlic, and red pepper flakes, season lightly with salt, and cook, stirring frequently, until onions are very soft but not browned, about 15 minutes; lower heat if needed to prevent browning
2. Add tomato paste and cook, stirring constantly, until paste is slightly darkened and very fragrant, about 5 minutes. Add tomatoes, crushing them with your hands as you add them, then add vodka and stir to combine
3. Bring to a simmer and cook, stirring occasionally, until sauce has thickened slightly, about 20 minutes
4. Add heavy cream and parmesan cheese. Stir to combine and simmer for another 10 minutes
5. Meanwhile, cook pasta according to package directions and heat meatballs
6. Toss pasta with sauce and serve with meatballs. Top with additional parmesan cheese
