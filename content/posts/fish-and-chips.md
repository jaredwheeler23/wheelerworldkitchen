## Ingredients

- 2 lbs cod or haddock fillets
- 4 large potatoes, cut into thick fries
- 1 1/2 cups flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 cup cold beer
- Salt and pepper
- Vegetable oil for frying
- Malt vinegar
- Tartar sauce

## Instructions

1. Soak potato fries in cold water for 30 minutes
2. Heat oil to 350°F
3. Fry potatoes in batches until golden, drain
4. Mix flour, baking powder, salt, and pepper
5. Whisk in cold beer until smooth
6. Increase oil temp to 375°F
7. Dip fish in batter, fry until golden (4-5 minutes)
8. Drain on paper towels
9. Serve with malt vinegar and tartar sauce
