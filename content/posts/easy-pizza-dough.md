## Ingredients

- 2.25 tsp (1 packet) active dry yeast
- 1 tbsp white sugar
- 1 cup warm (not hot) water
- 2.5 cups general purpose flour
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp olive oil (plus extra for greasing)

## Instructions

1. Mix sugar, yeast, and warm water in a bowl.  Let sit for 5-10 minutes until frothy.
2. Meanwhile combine rest of ingredients in a large bowl and stir well.
3. Combine all together and kneed well until properly mixed.
4. Move bowl of dough ball to a warm area and let sit for at least an hour or until it has doubled in size.
5. Ready to use for pizza!  Recomended to kneed and flatten on a floured surface.  Add a touch of flour or water to get the right consistency (not too wet, not too dry).
