## Ingredients

- 2 1/4 tsp (1 packet) active dry yeast
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 cup warm water (only 105°F, just a little over skin temp)
- 2-2.5 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp olive oil (plus extra for greasing)

## Instructions

**1. Activate the yeast:**
- In a small bowl, combine warm water, sugar, and yeast
- Let it sit for 5-10 minutes until it becomes foamy

**2. Make the dough:**
- In a large bowl, mix flour and salt
- Add the foamy yeast mixture and olive oil
- Stir until a dough forms. If it's sticky, add a bit more flour

**3. Knead:**
- Transfer dough to a floured surface and knead for about 5-7 minutes until smooth and elastic

**4. Let it rise:**
- Place dough in a lightly oiled bowl, cover with a towel or plastic wrap
- Let rise in a warm place for 1 hour, or until doubled in size

**5. Ready to use:**
- Punch it down, roll it out, and top with your favorite sauce, cheese, and toppings
- Bake at 425°F for 10-20 minutes, or until the crust is golden and bubbly/hot
