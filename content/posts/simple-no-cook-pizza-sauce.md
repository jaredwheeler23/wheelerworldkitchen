## Ingredients

- 1 (15 oz) can crushed tomatoes (or tomato puree for a smoother sauce)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp dried basil
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder (or 1 small garlic clove, finely minced)
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp sugar (optional, helps cut acidity)
- Black pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Mix everything together in a bowl
2. Taste and adjust seasoning
3. Let it sit for 10-15 minutes if you have time-flavors meld nicely

Done! No cooking needed-just spread it on your rolled-out dough.

## Notes

- It makes enough sauce for 2-3 pizzas
- You can store any leftovers in the fridge for up to a week or freeze it
