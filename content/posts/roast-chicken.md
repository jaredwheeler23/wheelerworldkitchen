## Ingredients

- 1 whole chicken (4-5 lbs)
- 4 tbsp butter, softened
- 2 lemons, halved
- 1 head garlic, halved
- Fresh herbs (rosemary, thyme)
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 425°F
2. Pat chicken dry with paper towels
3. Rub butter under and over the skin
4. Season generously with salt and pepper
5. Stuff cavity with lemon halves, garlic, and herbs
6. Tie legs together with kitchen twine
7. Roast for 1.5 hours until golden and internal temp reaches 165°F
8. Rest for 15 minutes before carving
