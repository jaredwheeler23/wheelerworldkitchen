## Ingredients

**Main:**
- 2 heads cauliflower, in 3/4 inch thick slabs
- 2-4 tbsp olive oil
- 1-2 tsp salt to taste
- 1-2 tsp black pepper to taste
- 1-2 tsp paprika to taste
- 0.5-1 tsp garlic powder to taste

## Instructions

1. Two options:
2. Option 1: grill it.  Might want to grill over tinfoil as edges can fall off and get lost.  Heat up grill and flip after 5 minutes and remove when cooked through.
3. Option 2: Oven.  Preheat to 425 F and bake for 15 minutes or until cooked through.
