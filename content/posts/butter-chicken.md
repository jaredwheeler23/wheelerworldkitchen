## Ingredients

- 2 lbs chicken thighs, cubed
- 1 cup yogurt
- 2 tbsp garam masala
- 1 tbsp turmeric
- 1 onion, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp ginger, minced
- 28 oz can crushed tomatoes
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 4 tbsp butter
- Fresh cilantro
- Salt

## Instructions

1. Marinate chicken in yogurt, garam masala, and turmeric for 30 minutes
2. Sauté onion, garlic, and ginger in butter
3. Add marinated chicken, cook until browned
4. Add crushed tomatoes, simmer 20 minutes
5. Stir in heavy cream
6. Simmer 10 more minutes
7. Garnish with cilantro
8. Serve with naan or rice
