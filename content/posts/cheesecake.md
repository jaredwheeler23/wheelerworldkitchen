## Ingredients

**Crust:**
- 2 cups graham cracker crumbs
- 1/2 cup melted butter
- 1/4 cup sugar

**Filling:**
- 32 oz cream cheese, softened
- 1 1/4 cups sugar
- 4 eggs
- 1 cup sour cream
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/4 cup flour

## Instructions

1. Mix graham cracker crumbs, melted butter, and sugar
2. Press into bottom of 9-inch springform pan
3. Bake crust at 325°F for 10 minutes
4. Beat cream cheese until fluffy
5. Add sugar, beat until smooth
6. Add eggs one at a time
7. Mix in sour cream, vanilla, and flour
8. Pour over crust
9. Bake at 325°F for 55-60 minutes (center should jiggle slightly)
10. Turn off oven, leave cheesecake inside for 1 hour
11. Refrigerate at least 4 hours before serving
