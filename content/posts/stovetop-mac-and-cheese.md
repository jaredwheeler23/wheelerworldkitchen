## Ingredients

- 1 lb elbow macaroni
- 4 tbsp butter
- 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 3 cups whole milk, warmed
- 3 cups shredded sharp cheddar cheese
- 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp mustard powder (optional but recommended)
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Pinch of nutmeg (optional)

## Instructions

1. Cook macaroni according to package directions until al dente. Drain and set aside
2. In a large pot over medium heat, melt butter
3. Whisk in flour and cook for 1-2 minutes, stirring constantly, to create a roux
4. Slowly add warm milk, whisking constantly to avoid lumps. Continue whisking until mixture thickens, about 5 minutes
5. Reduce heat to low. Add garlic powder, mustard powder, salt, pepper, and nutmeg. Stir to combine
6. Add shredded cheeses a handful at a time, stirring until melted and smooth before adding more
7. Once all cheese is melted and sauce is smooth, add cooked macaroni. Stir until pasta is well coated
8. Taste and adjust seasonings. If too thick, add a splash of milk. If too thin, simmer for a few more minutes
9. Serve immediately while hot and creamy

## Notes

- For extra richness, stir in 4 oz cream cheese with the cheddar
- Can mix up cheese varieties: try gruyere, fontina, or pepper jack
- Top with breadcrumbs and broil for 2 minutes for a crispy top
