## Ingredients

- 2 lbs chicken thighs
- 1/2 cup soy sauce
- 1/4 cup mirin
- 1/4 cup sake or white wine
- 3 tbsp sugar
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp ginger, minced
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- Sesame seeds
- Green onions

## Instructions

1. Mix soy sauce, mirin, sake, sugar, garlic, and ginger
2. Marinate chicken for 15 minutes
3. Remove chicken, reserve marinade
4. Grill or pan-fry chicken until cooked through
5. Simmer reserved marinade with cornstarch until thickened
6. Brush glaze over cooked chicken
7. Garnish with sesame seeds and green onions
8. Serve over rice
