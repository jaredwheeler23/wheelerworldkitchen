## Ingredients

- 1 lb spaghetti
- 6 oz pancetta or bacon, diced
- 4 eggs
- 1 cup Parmesan cheese, grated
- Black pepper
- Salt

## Instructions

1. Cook spaghetti in salted boiling water until al dente
2. While pasta cooks, fry pancetta until crispy
3. Beat eggs with Parmesan and lots of black pepper
4. Reserve 1 cup pasta water, then drain pasta
5. Remove pan from heat, add hot pasta to pancetta
6. Quickly stir in egg mixture, adding pasta water to create creamy sauce
7. Serve immediately with extra Parmesan and pepper
