## Ingredients

- 4 cups cooked rice (day-old works best)
- 2 chicken breasts, diced
- 3 eggs, beaten
- 1 cup mixed vegetables (peas, carrots, corn)
- 3 green onions, sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp ginger, minced
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tsp sesame oil
- Vegetable oil

## Instructions

1. Heat wok over high heat with oil
2. Scramble eggs, set aside
3. Stir-fry chicken until cooked through, set aside
4. Add vegetables, garlic, and ginger, stir-fry 2 minutes
5. Add rice, breaking up clumps
6. Add soy sauce and sesame oil
7. Return chicken and eggs to wok, toss to combine
8. Top with green onions
