# Bangers and Mash with Guinness Gravy

Classic British pub food featuring beer bratwurst, creamy mashed potatoes, and rich Guinness gravy.

## Ingredients

### Sausage
- 1 package beer brauts (1 lb or more)

### Gravy
- 4 tbsp butter
- 1 med yellow onion, thinly sliced
- 4 tsp flour
- 1 bottle guinness (11 oz)
- 1 can beef broth (or any broth)
- 1 tbsp dijon mustard
- 1 tbsp worcestershire sauce
- 2 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tsp salt
- Ground pepper to taste

### Mashed Potatoes
- 1-2 pounds of taters
- Dealers choice on your recipe here. Use butter!

## Instructions

1. Cook sausage as you want. Recommended to do on grill or frying pan covered.

2. Gravy: in large skillet, over medium high heat, cook and stir onions in butter for about 20 minutes until caramelized.

3. Add flour, cook and stir for a few minutes until golden brown.

4. Add guinness, broth, dijon, worcester, sugar, salt, and pepper. Reduce heat and simmer for 15-20 minutes until thickened.

5. Assemble: bangers + mash + gravy

## Notes

Optional, cook chicken thighs or legs same way as brauts and it becomes bangers, flappers and mash.
