## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 small onion diced
- 4 cloves garlic minced
- 3/4 cup quick oats
- 15 oz can white beans (cannellini) rinsed and drained
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 2 tbsp italian seasoning
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper

## Instructions

1. In large pan, heat oil and saute the garlic and onions until softened.
2. transfer above and rest of ingredients to a food processor and process until a coarse mixture.
3. (optional): place in freezer for 20 minutes (or fridge for an hour) to harden ingredients a bit and make easier for next step.
4. With your hands, create ~15 balls and line them on a baking sheet.
5. In preheated oven, bake for 15 minutes at 350F.
6. Serve with your favorite pasta dish.

## Notes

- After baking, do not try to cook further in marinara sauce.  That will break them apart.  Server over pasta and then pour marinara over them.  Flavor is fine and doesn't need extra cooking in the sauce.
