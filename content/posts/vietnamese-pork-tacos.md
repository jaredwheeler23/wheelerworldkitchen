## Ingredients

**Pickled vegetables:**
- 1/2 cup rice vinegar
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 carrot, julienned
- 1/2 daikon radish, julienned
- 1/4 red onion, thinly sliced

**Pork:**
- 1.5 lbs pork tenderloin or boneless pork chops, thinly sliced
- 2 stalks lemongrass, minced (use only tender white parts)
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1/2 tsp black pepper

**Sauce:**
- 1/4 cup mayonnaise
- 1 tbsp sriracha
- 1 tsp lime juice

**Assembly:**
- 8 small flour or corn tortillas
- Fresh cilantro leaves
- Fresh mint leaves
- Fresh basil leaves
- Lime wedges
- Sliced jalapeños (optional)
- Crushed peanuts (optional)

## Instructions

1. Make pickled vegetables: In a bowl, whisk together rice vinegar, sugar, and salt until dissolved. Add carrot, daikon, and red onion. Let sit for at least 20 minutes
2. Marinate pork: In a bowl, combine lemongrass, garlic, fish sauce, soy sauce, brown sugar, oil, and black pepper. Add sliced pork and toss to coat. Marinate for 15-30 minutes
3. Make sauce: Mix mayonnaise, sriracha, and lime juice in a small bowl. Set aside
4. Heat a large skillet or grill pan over high heat. Cook pork in batches for 2-3 minutes per side until caramelized and cooked through
5. Warm tortillas in a dry skillet or over an open flame
6. Assemble tacos: Place cooked pork on tortillas, top with pickled vegetables, drizzle with sauce, and add fresh herbs. Garnish with crushed peanuts if desired. Serve with lime wedges and jalapeños

## Notes

- Lemongrass can be found in Asian grocery stores. If unavailable, substitute with 1 tsp lemon zest
- For extra flavor, grill the pork over charcoal
- Pickled vegetables can be made up to 3 days ahead
