## Ingredients

- Bag of lentils (the small one at store works)
- Large can of tomato sauce, 29 oz
- Water (eyeball it)
- 1 tsp baking soda or not if you want the toots
- Few tsp salt as needed
- Few tsp pepper as desired
- 1-2 tbsp oregano
- 1 tbsp parsley
- Few carrots, diced small
- 1 large yellow onion, diced small
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- Ditalini pasta (or other small pasta), 1 lb

## Instructions

1. Boil lentils in pot and drain (do not cook, this is to take out raw taste and start the process). Put lentils to side briefly
2. In same pot, saute garlic, carrot, and onion until cooked and/or golden for garlic
3. Add can of tomato sauce (try adding a few diced small fresh tomatoes too)
4. Fill at least another can of water and add to pot, add lentils back too. Add more water as you cook, as needed. Likely another can or two worth. Whatever you see, add more as the lentils (and eventually the pasta after it is cooked) will soak it up
5. Add seasonings
6. Cook at least one hour if not more (goal is soft lentils), while lentils are cooking, cook the pasta in separate pot
7. Combine all to serve
