## Ingredients

- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 cups short grain rice (arborio or bomba rice)
- 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes
- 3 cups seafood or chicken stock
- 1/2 tsp saffron threads (or 1 tsp turmeric as substitute)
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1 lb mussels, scrubbed and debearded
- 1/2 lb calamari rings (optional)
- 1 cup frozen peas
- Lemon wedges for serving
- Fresh parsley, chopped

## Instructions

1. Set pressure cooker to sauté mode. Heat olive oil and cook onion and bell pepper until softened, about 5 minutes
2. Add garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant
3. Stir in rice and cook for 2 minutes, stirring to coat with oil
4. Add diced tomatoes, stock, saffron (or turmeric), paprika, salt, and pepper. Stir well
5. Close lid and set to high pressure for 6 minutes
6. When done, quick release the pressure
7. Open lid and switch back to sauté mode. Stir in shrimp, mussels, calamari (if using), and peas
8. Cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring gently, until shrimp are pink and cooked through and mussels have opened (discard any that don't open)
9. Turn off heat and let stand for 5 minutes
10. Garnish with fresh parsley and serve with lemon wedges

## Notes

- Traditional paella develops a crispy bottom layer called "socarrat" - to achieve this, let the paella sit on sauté mode for an extra 3-4 minutes without stirring
- Can substitute or add other seafood like clams, scallops, or firm white fish
