## Ingredients

1. 2 cups all purpose flour
2. 3 large eggs
3. 1/2 tsp salt
4. 1/2 tbsp olive oil

## Instructions

1. Place flour on clean, flat surface and make a nest. Add eggs, oil and salt to center and use fork to break/mix things in while keeping flour walls intact
2. Now use hands to gently bring the flour inward to incorporate
3. Work ball with your hands to bring it together and continue to knead for 10 minutes or so. (it will feel dry at first but be patient). It should beome conhesive and smooth. If still dry and kneaded to death, add a smidge of water to finalize it. IF too sticky, add a smidge of flour
4. Let dough rest at room temp for 30 minutes
5. Now, proceed to use dough such as with pasta attachment on kitchen aid or something
