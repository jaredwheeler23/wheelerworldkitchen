## Ingredients

- 1 head green cabbage, shredded
- 2 carrots, grated
- 1 cup mayonnaise
- 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp celery seed
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Combine shredded cabbage and carrots in large bowl
2. In separate bowl, whisk together mayo, vinegar, sugar, and celery seed
3. Pour dressing over cabbage mixture
4. Toss until well coated
5. Season with salt and pepper
6. Refrigerate at least 1 hour before serving
