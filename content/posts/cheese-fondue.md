## Ingredients

- 1/2 lb gruyere, grated
- 1/2 lb fontina, grated
- 1/2 lb gouda, grated
- 2 tbsp cornstarch
- 1 cup white sauvignon blanc wine
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp brandy or whiskey or cooking sherry
- 1 tsp dijon mustard or stone ground mustard
- 1/4 tsp nutmeg

**Dipping ideas:**
- Bread cubes (french bread or sourdough)
- Steamed broccoli florets
- Boiled small potatoes
- Apple slices
- Pear slices
- Pan-seared sausage slices

## Instructions

1. Grate all cheeses and combine with cornstarch in a large bowl. Toss to coat evenly
2. In fondue pot or heavy-bottomed saucepan, bring wine, garlic, and lemon juice to a simmer for 1-2 minutes
3. Reduce heat to low. Slowly add the cheese mixture a handful at a time, stirring constantly in a figure-8 pattern until smooth before adding more
4. Once all cheese is melted and smooth, stir in brandy, mustard, and nutmeg
5. Keep fondue warm over low heat (or fondue burner). The fondue should be softly bubbling
6. If fondue gets too thick, add a splash of wine. If too thin, add more grated cheese mixed with cornstarch
7. Serve with dipping items arranged on a platter. Use fondue forks for dipping

## Notes

- Don't let the fondue boil or the cheese will separate
- Stir frequently to prevent sticking and burning
- If cheese separates, whisk in 1 tsp lemon juice or cornstarch slurry to bring it back together
- Leftover fondue can be refrigerated and reheated gently, adding wine as needed
