Copycat Chipotle Style Pan Cooked Veggies

Greek Rice with Dill and Lemon

Ingredients 2 tbsp olive oil 1 small onion diced small 3 cloves garlic minced 1.5 cups uncooked white rice 3 cups chicken broth 1 tbsp fresh dill (or less dried) 1-2 tbsp lemon juice Instructions Saute over med heat onion and garlic until translucent Add rice to pan and saute for 5 minutes stirring frequently Either transfer to a pot to cook rice in or move this all to a rice cooker. Regardless, pour in chicken broth, mix, and simmer until rice is cooked Add in dill, lemon juice. Mix and serve

Mexican Pasta

Ingredients 1 lb pasta (cooked to package directions) 4 tbsp butter 4 cloves garlic, minced 1.5 cups heavy cream 0.5 cup milk (almond, oat or whole) 3 tsp salt 2 tsp lime juice or more to taste 8 bay leaves 1/2 cup queso fresco (or mozzarella works too) Instructions In a sauce pan saute garlic in butter until fragrant Add the rest of the ingredients to the bay leaves. Simmer for 5+ minutes. Stirring often. It should thicken a smidge. Remove bay leaves if you can Add to the cooked pasta and stir well, add queso fresco too Notes Serve with grilled shrimp or something tasty The heavy cream, salt and lime juice is a combo to simulate Mexican cream. You can just use that instead if you got it

Mexican Rice

Ingredients 1 1/2 cups long grain white rice 1/4 cup oil (vegetable or canola oil) 3 cloves garlic, minced 1/2 medium onion, finely diced 1 tsp cumin 1 tsp chili powder 1/3 cup tomato sauce 2 teaspoons tomato bouillon granulated, or cubes (or 1 tbsp ketchup) 2 teaspoon salt 1 carrot, diced small 1/2 cup peas (frozen or fresh) 3 cups water 1-3 whole serrano peppers, optional Instructions In a large saucepan over medium-high heat, add the oil. Once hot, add the rice and stir to combine. Cook over medium heat, stirring frequently, until the rice is lightly golden brown all over (about 10 minutes) Add tomato sauce, garlic, diced onion, cumin, and chili powder to the pan. Stir. Add tomato bullion, salt, carrots, peas, and water (and serrano peppers, if using. They add a little flavor, but not spice.) Bring to a boil, then cover, reduce heat to low and cook for about 20 minutes or until the water is completely absorbed. Remove from heat and allow to rest for 5 minutes before fluffing with a fork Taste test. Spruce up with more salt, pepper, and cholula as necessary

Potato Salad

Ingredients 3 pounds gold potatoes 4 hard boiled eggs 1 cup mayonnaise 1/4 cup buttermilk (or just milk in a pinch) 2 tbsp yellow mustard 2 dill pickles diced well Salt and pepper to taste 2 ribs celery sliced relatively small 1/4 cup red onion diced small Instructions Simmer potatoes for 20 minutes. Note: if you have potatoes that aren’t small or med, chop them in half before adding to pot. Large ones can take longer to boil Remove water from pan and return to stove under same simmer settings for 10-15 minutes to steam them (add a splash of water if they dry out completely) Optionally peel potatoes. I do not Make dressing: remove the yolk from the hard boiled eggs and mash in large bowl. Then chop the whites and add on top of them (do not mash the whites). Add rest of ingredients and stir well. Add a splash of pickle juice and stir well Chop the potatoes when done and add to the bowl you just made Let chill in the fridge. You can serve whenever but it is best if you let it chill and the flavors meld in the fridge for at least a couple hours or better overnight

Spaghetti Squash Pasta

Ingredients 1 large Spaghetti Squash Salt Olive oil Instructions Microwave the squash for 2 minutes (to make it easier to cut) and then cut in half lengthwise Drizzle on cut side of each half olive oil and salt Flip over onto a baking pan (with tinfoil) and bake for 45 minutes at 400°F

Zesty Beans for Mexican Dishes

Ingredients 1 can black beans (rinsed and drained very well) 1/2 small can chipotles in adobo sauce 3 large garlic cloves minced 1/2 tsp cumin 1/2 tsp chili powder 1 tsp salt 3 tbsp lime juice Can of HOT rotel tomatoes (drained very well and rinsed) Instructions With a smidge of olive oil, saute the garlic until fragrant. Add chipotles and adobo sauce (about half a small can). Saute a smidge longer Add rest of ingredients and bring to simmer. Simmer for 20 minutes Using an immersion blender (ideally) smooth the beans in the pot. (You can also remove and use a food processor but that is messy… immersion blender is best here) Serve inside a burrito or with your favorite mexican dishes
