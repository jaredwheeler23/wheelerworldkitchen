Chicken Burritos

Ingredients 2 lbs chicken breast, diced 2 cups cooked rice 1 can black beans, drained 1 bell pepper, diced 1 onion, diced 2 tsp cumin 1 tsp chili powder 6 large flour tortillas Cheese, sour cream, guacamole, salsa Instructions Cook chicken in large skillet until golden Add bell pepper and onion, cook until softened Stir in rice, beans, cumin, and chili powder Warm tortillas in microwave Divide filling among tortillas Add cheese and desired toppings Fold in sides and roll up tightly Optional: Grill seam-side down until golden

Chicken Fried Rice

Ingredients 4 cups cooked rice (day-old works best) 2 chicken breasts, diced 3 eggs, beaten 1 cup mixed vegetables (peas, carrots, corn) 3 green onions, sliced 3 cloves garlic, minced 1 tbsp ginger, minced 3 tbsp soy sauce 2 tsp sesame oil Vegetable oil Instructions Heat wok over high heat with oil Scramble eggs, set aside Stir-fry chicken until cooked through, set aside Add vegetables, garlic, and ginger, stir-fry 2 minutes Add rice, breaking up clumps Add soy sauce and sesame oil Return chicken and eggs to wok, toss to combine Top with green onions

Mushroom Risotto

Ingredients 1.5 cups Arborio rice 8 oz mushrooms, sliced 6 cups chicken or vegetable broth, warmed 1 onion, finely diced 3 cloves garlic, minced 1/2 cup white wine 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese 3 tbsp butter 2 tbsp olive oil Fresh parsley Salt and pepper Instructions Sauté mushrooms in butter until golden, set aside In same pot, heat olive oil and cook onion until soft Add garlic and rice, stir for 2 minutes Add wine and stir until absorbed Add broth one ladle at a time, stirring constantly Continue adding broth until rice is creamy and al dente (about 25 minutes) Stir in mushrooms, Parmesan, and remaining butter Season with salt and pepper, garnish with parsley
