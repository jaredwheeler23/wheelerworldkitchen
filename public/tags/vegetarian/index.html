Autumn Pot Pie (Christmas Veggie Pot Pie)

Autumn Pot Pie (Christmas Veggie Pot Pie) A stunning vegetarian pot pie perfect for the holidays, featuring butternut squash and a homemade cheddar crust. Ingredients Cheddar Pastry 2 cups all purpose white flour 1/2 tsp salt 1/2 cup cold butter, diced 1 cup chilled, grated sharp cheddar cheese (buy a bag of shredded cheese) 4-5 tbsp ice water Vegetable Filling 1 tbsp vegetable oil 4 cups chopped leeks (use only white and light green parts of 2 medium leeks) 2 bay leaves 1 tsp dried thyme 1 tsp dried sage (or 1 tbsp fresh) 1/2 tsp salt 2 cups green beans (cut into 1 in. lengths) 3/4 cup vegetable stock 3 cups chopped green cabbage 3 cups cubed butternut squash 3 cups sliced mushrooms Cheese Sauce 3 tbsp butter 1/4 cup all purpose white flour 1.5 cups warmed vegetable stock 1/2 tsp dijon mustard or similar 1/2 cup grated cheese, parmesan works Salt and pepper to taste Instructions Start with cheddar pastry. Use a food processor and briefly pulse the flour, salt, and butter until the mixture has lumps about the size of peas. Add cheddar and pulse a few times until the mixture resembles a coarse meal. Guacamole

Ingredients 4 ripe avocados 1 lime, juiced 1/2 red onion, finely diced 1 jalapeño, seeded and minced 2 Roma tomatoes, diced 1/4 cup fresh cilantro, chopped Salt and pepper Tortilla chips for serving Instructions Cut avocados in half, remove pit Scoop flesh into bowl and mash with fork Add lime juice immediately to prevent browning Fold in onion, jalapeño, tomatoes, and cilantro Season with salt and pepper to taste Serve immediately with tortilla chips

Margherita Pizza

Ingredients For dough: 3 cups flour 1 packet active dry yeast 1 cup warm water 2 tbsp olive oil 1 tsp sugar 1 tsp salt For topping: 1 cup crushed tomatoes 8 oz fresh mozzarella, sliced Fresh basil leaves Olive oil Salt Instructions Mix yeast with warm water and sugar, let sit 5 minutes Combine flour and salt, add yeast mixture and olive oil Knead until smooth, let rise 1.5 hours Divide dough in half, roll into circles Spread crushed tomatoes on dough Top with mozzarella slices Drizzle with olive oil, sprinkle with salt Bake at 475°F for 12-15 minutes until crust is golden Top with fresh basil after baking

Mushroom Risotto

Ingredients 1.5 cups Arborio rice 8 oz mushrooms, sliced 6 cups chicken or vegetable broth, warmed 1 onion, finely diced 3 cloves garlic, minced 1/2 cup white wine 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese 3 tbsp butter 2 tbsp olive oil Fresh parsley Salt and pepper Instructions Sauté mushrooms in butter until golden, set aside In same pot, heat olive oil and cook onion until soft Add garlic and rice, stir for 2 minutes Add wine and stir until absorbed Add broth one ladle at a time, stirring constantly Continue adding broth until rice is creamy and al dente (about 25 minutes) Stir in mushrooms, Parmesan, and remaining butter Season with salt and pepper, garnish with parsley
