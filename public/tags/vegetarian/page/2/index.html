Penne Arrabbiata

Ingredients 1 lb penne pasta 3 tbsp olive oil 6 cloves garlic, minced 1 tsp red pepper flakes (adjust to taste) 28 oz can crushed tomatoes 1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped Salt Parmesan cheese for serving Instructions Cook penne in salted boiling water until al dente Heat olive oil in large skillet Sauté garlic and red pepper flakes until fragrant Add crushed tomatoes and salt Simmer for 15 minutes Toss cooked pasta with sauce Garnish with parsley and Parmesan

Veggie Shepherds Pie

Veggie Shepherds Pie A hearty vegetarian version of shepherd’s pie with lentils, vegetables, and kale-infused mashed potatoes. Ingredients Filling 2 tbsp vegetable oil 1 large onion, diced small 3 large carrots, diced small 1 tsp dried thyme 1 cup brown or green lentils 4 tbsp red wine (or less of red wine vinegar) 1.5-2 cups water 1-2 tsp salt Mashed Potatoes 2-3 pounds yukon gold potatoes Few tsp salt 3 large garlic cloves, peeled but left whole Butter or olive oil (if vegetarian or making vegan) Black pepper 4 cups chopped kale (2 bunches) 2 cloves garlic, chopped roughly Parmesan cheese Instructions Filling In small sauce pan on medium heat, saute onion, carrot and thyme until softened (about 5 minutes). Add lentils and wine and stir well, bring to lively simmer. Add 1.5 cups water and bring back to simmer. Lower heat, cover and cook until tender (about 40 minutes). Add more water if necessary. Taste at end and add salt if needed. Set aside. Mashed Potatoes Get a large/wide soup pot and fill one layer (only one layer) densely with potatoes. Then add water until just the last millimeter of potato is above the water line.
