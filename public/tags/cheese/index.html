Chicken Parmesan

Ingredients 4 chicken breasts, pounded thin 2 cups breadcrumbs 1 cup Parmesan cheese, grated 2 eggs, beaten 1 cup flour 2 cups marinara sauce 2 cups mozzarella cheese, shredded Fresh basil Salt and pepper Olive oil for frying Instructions Set up breading station: flour, beaten eggs, breadcrumbs mixed with Parmesan Season chicken with salt and pepper Dredge in flour, dip in egg, coat with breadcrumb mixture Fry in olive oil until golden on both sides Place chicken in baking dish, top with marinara and mozzarella Bake at 400°F for 15-20 minutes until cheese is melted Garnish with fresh basil Serve over pasta

Creamy Mac and Cheese

Ingredients 1 lb elbow macaroni 4 tbsp butter 4 tbsp flour 3 cups milk 3 cups shredded cheddar cheese 1 cup shredded mozzarella Salt, pepper, and paprika 1 cup breadcrumbs 2 tbsp melted butter Instructions Cook macaroni according to package directions, drain In large pot, melt butter and whisk in flour Gradually add milk, whisking constantly Cook until thickened, about 5 minutes Remove from heat, stir in cheeses until melted Add cooked macaroni, season with salt, pepper, and paprika Transfer to greased baking dish Mix breadcrumbs with melted butter, sprinkle on top Bake at 375°F for 25-30 minutes until golden

Margherita Pizza

Ingredients For dough: 3 cups flour 1 packet active dry yeast 1 cup warm water 2 tbsp olive oil 1 tsp sugar 1 tsp salt For topping: 1 cup crushed tomatoes 8 oz fresh mozzarella, sliced Fresh basil leaves Olive oil Salt Instructions Mix yeast with warm water and sugar, let sit 5 minutes Combine flour and salt, add yeast mixture and olive oil Knead until smooth, let rise 1.5 hours Divide dough in half, roll into circles Spread crushed tomatoes on dough Top with mozzarella slices Drizzle with olive oil, sprinkle with salt Bake at 475°F for 12-15 minutes until crust is golden Top with fresh basil after baking

Palak Paneer

Ingredients 1 lb paneer cheese, cubed 1 lb fresh spinach 1 onion, diced 4 cloves garlic, minced 1 tbsp ginger, minced 2 tomatoes, diced 1 tsp cumin seeds 1 tsp garam masala 1/2 tsp turmeric 1/2 cup cream Salt Vegetable oil Instructions Blanch spinach, blend into smooth paste Fry paneer cubes until golden, set aside Sauté cumin seeds, onion, garlic, and ginger Add tomatoes and spices, cook until soft Add spinach puree, simmer 10 minutes Stir in cream Add fried paneer Serve with naan or rice
