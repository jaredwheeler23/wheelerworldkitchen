Butter Chicken

Ingredients 2 lbs chicken thighs, cubed 1 cup yogurt 2 tbsp garam masala 1 tbsp turmeric 1 onion, diced 4 cloves garlic, minced 1 tbsp ginger, minced 28 oz can crushed tomatoes 1 cup heavy cream 4 tbsp butter Fresh cilantro Salt Instructions Marinate chicken in yogurt, garam masala, and turmeric for 30 minutes Sauté onion, garlic, and ginger in butter Add marinated chicken, cook until browned Add crushed tomatoes, simmer 20 minutes Stir in heavy cream Simmer 10 more minutes Garnish with cilantro Serve with naan or rice

Chile Verde

Ingredients 3 lbs pork shoulder, cubed 2 lbs tomatillos, husked and halved 2 poblano peppers, roasted and peeled 2 jalapeños 1 onion, quartered 4 cloves garlic 1 cup chicken broth 1 tsp cumin Fresh cilantro Salt and pepper Instructions Roast tomatillos and peppers under broiler until charred Blend roasted vegetables with onion, garlic, and broth Brown pork in batches in Dutch oven Add blended sauce and cumin Simmer covered for 4 hours until pork is tender Season with salt and pepper Garnish with fresh cilantro Serve with rice or in tortillas

Penne Arrabbiata

Ingredients 1 lb penne pasta 3 tbsp olive oil 6 cloves garlic, minced 1 tsp red pepper flakes (adjust to taste) 28 oz can crushed tomatoes 1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped Salt Parmesan cheese for serving Instructions Cook penne in salted boiling water until al dente Heat olive oil in large skillet Sauté garlic and red pepper flakes until fragrant Add crushed tomatoes and salt Simmer for 15 minutes Toss cooked pasta with sauce Garnish with parsley and Parmesan
