Beef and Broccoli

Ingredients 1.5 lbs flank steak, sliced thin 1 lb broccoli florets 4 cloves garlic, minced 1 tbsp ginger, minced 1/4 cup soy sauce 2 tbsp oyster sauce 1 tbsp cornstarch 1 tbsp brown sugar 1/2 cup beef broth Sesame oil Vegetable oil Instructions Mix soy sauce, oyster sauce, cornstarch, sugar, and broth Marinate beef in half the sauce for 15 minutes Blanch broccoli in boiling water for 2 minutes, drain Heat wok with oil over high heat Stir-fry beef until browned, set aside Add garlic and ginger, cook 30 seconds Add broccoli and remaining sauce Return beef to wok, toss until sauce thickens Drizzle with sesame oil Serve over rice

Beef Fajitas

Ingredients 1.5 lbs flank steak, sliced thin 3 bell peppers, sliced 1 large onion, sliced 3 tbsp lime juice 2 tsp cumin 2 tsp chili powder 1 tsp paprika Salt and pepper 8 flour tortillas Sour cream, guacamole, salsa Instructions Marinate beef in lime juice and spices for 30 minutes Heat oil in large skillet over high heat Cook beef in batches until browned, set aside Add peppers and onions, cook until tender-crisp Return beef to skillet, toss to combine Warm tortillas Serve beef mixture in tortillas with desired toppings

Chicken Fried Rice

Ingredients 4 cups cooked rice (day-old works best) 2 chicken breasts, diced 3 eggs, beaten 1 cup mixed vegetables (peas, carrots, corn) 3 green onions, sliced 3 cloves garlic, minced 1 tbsp ginger, minced 3 tbsp soy sauce 2 tsp sesame oil Vegetable oil Instructions Heat wok over high heat with oil Scramble eggs, set aside Stir-fry chicken until cooked through, set aside Add vegetables, garlic, and ginger, stir-fry 2 minutes Add rice, breaking up clumps Add soy sauce and sesame oil Return chicken and eggs to wok, toss to combine Top with green onions

Chicken Teriyaki

Ingredients 2 lbs chicken thighs 1/2 cup soy sauce 1/4 cup mirin 1/4 cup sake or white wine 3 tbsp sugar 2 cloves garlic, minced 1 tbsp ginger, minced 1 tbsp cornstarch Sesame seeds Green onions Instructions Mix soy sauce, mirin, sake, sugar, garlic, and ginger Marinate chicken for 15 minutes Remove chicken, reserve marinade Grill or pan-fry chicken until cooked through Simmer reserved marinade with cornstarch until thickened Brush glaze over cooked chicken Garnish with sesame seeds and green onions Serve over rice

Chocolate Lava Cakes

Ingredients 6 oz dark chocolate, chopped 1/2 cup butter 2 eggs 2 egg yolks 1/4 cup sugar 2 tbsp flour Pinch of salt Butter and cocoa for ramekins Vanilla ice cream for serving Instructions Butter and cocoa dust 4 ramekins Melt chocolate and butter together Whisk eggs, egg yolks, and sugar until thick Fold in melted chocolate Gently fold in flour and salt Divide among ramekins Bake at 425°F for 12-14 minutes Centers should be soft Let stand 1 minute, then invert onto plates Serve immediately with vanilla ice cream

Classic Beef Tacos

Ingredients 2 lbs ground beef 1 onion, diced 3 cloves garlic, minced 2 tbsp chili powder 1 tsp cumin 1 tsp paprika Salt and pepper 12 taco shells Toppings: lettuce, tomatoes, cheese, sour cream, salsa Instructions Brown ground beef in large skillet over medium-high heat Add onion and garlic, cook until softened Drain excess fat Add spices and 1/2 cup water Simmer for 10 minutes until mixture thickens Warm taco shells according to package directions Fill shells with beef and desired toppings

Classic Coleslaw

Ingredients 1 head green cabbage, shredded 2 carrots, grated 1 cup mayonnaise 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar 1 tbsp sugar 1 tsp celery seed Salt and pepper Instructions Combine shredded cabbage and carrots in large bowl In separate bowl, whisk together mayo, vinegar, sugar, and celery seed Pour dressing over cabbage mixture Toss until well coated Season with salt and pepper Refrigerate at least 1 hour before serving
