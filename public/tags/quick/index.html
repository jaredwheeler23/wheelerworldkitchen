Asian Noodle Bowl (Not Soup)

Ingredients Sauce: 1/4 cup soy sauce 2 tsp grated lime zest 1/4 cup fresh lime juice 1/4 cup brown sugar 1/2 tsp minced jalapeño (or more), or chinese chili paste 2 tsp crushed or minced garlic Toppings: 1/4 cup chopped basil 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro 1 cup roasted peanuts or almonds Main: 1/4 cup vegetable oil 2 cups sliced scallions (cut into 1" pieces) 6 cups thinly sliced broccoli 2 cups chopped fresh tomatoes 2 tbsp grated ginger 8 oz rice stick noodles 1 tbsp dark sesame oil 4 large eggs, beaten Instructions Make the sauce, in a bowl whisk together sauce ingredients. Set sauce on the table Bring water to boil and cook noodles according to package directions Heat oil in wok or large skillet and saute scallions until soft. Stir in broccoli and cook for a few minutes until color deepens. Stir in the tomatoes and ginger and cover for about 7 minutes, or at least until broccoli is tender At same time, heat dark sesame oil in a skillet, pour in the beaten eggs and cook until the edges are set and then flip to cook the opposite side. Remove the eggs from the heat and slice into thin strips Serve each person a large bowl filled with a mix of noodles, topping and strips of fried egg. They, at the table, top with sauce, fresh herbs, and peanuts/almonds

Chickpea Curry, Quick, Easy and Light

Ingredients 2 tbsp coconut oil 1 large yellow onion diced well 1, 15-oz, can canned diced tomatoes (the one with jalapeno in it is better) 1, 16-oz, can chickpeas (or garbanzo beans to the Tucsonan) 5 garlic cloves minced 1.5-2 tbsp garam masala 1-1.5 tsp cury powder 1/4 tsp cumin 1 can coconut milk 2 tsp flour (coconut preferred) optionally if you want to thicken it a smidge 1-2 tbsp lime juice, do this to taste but this is a critical step and do NOT skip Salt and pepper to taste Instructions In oil saute in large pot onions and tomatoes until onions are soft stirring occasionally. ~10 minutes. Add rest of ingredients up to and including the flour, stir very well. It helps to add some at a time and stir them in, especially the spices. Bring to simmer, reduce heat, and simmer for at least 10 minutes. Add lime, salt, pepper to taste and serve. Do not forget the lime.

Creamy Mushroom Quesadillas

Ingredients Olive oil 2 cups diced well onions 1 package sliced mushrooms (12 oz) Salt to taste Pepper to taste 1 tsp cumin 1 tsp chili powder 3 oz cream cheese 1 cup shredded cheese (mexican or cheddar) Package of huge flour tortillas (need 4 of them) Salsa at the table Instructions Warm oil in skillet on medium high heat and saute onions until soft and translucent (~5 min) Stir in mushrooms, seasonings, cover and cook for a few more minutes on medium heat Uncover and continue to cook until mushroom juices have evaporated Turn off heat and stir in cheeses. Move to bowl Make quesadillas by adding 1/4 of mixture to one side of tortillas. Then fold tortilla over to make one. Do this 4 times In same skillet with a little more oil, cook quesadillas on each side until brown Serve with salsa, fresh cut tomatoes, and avocado if you have it

Miso, Noodle, Peanut Soup

Ingredients Broth: 2 cm fresh ginger, finely grated 2 green onions, minced 1.5 cups water 2 tbsp miso paste (either white or red, dealers choice) 2 tbsp peanut butter no sugar added (or almond or similar, get unsweetened if possible) Noodles: 1 packet ramen, or equivalent in rice noodles, or soba or udon or whatever available For serving: 1 sheet nori (seaweed) 1 red chili, finely sliced (or red chili flakes work fine) 2 tbsp fresh coriander (or 1-2 tbsp ground coriander works) 2 tsp black or white sesame seeds Instructions Cook the noodles separate as per package. Then place in serving bowl Place ginger and onion in small sauce pan with tiny bit of oil. Turn on the heat and just start to cook/sizzle, then add the miso and peanut butter along with the water. Whisk together and slowly bring to simmer. The instant it starts simmering, take off the heat and pour in serving bowl with the noodles Add the servings ingredients to the bowl and serve immediately

Texas Style Quesadillas

Example photo for half size quesadillas Ingredients 1 package LARGE Flour Tortillas (comes in pack of 8 or 10) Few tbsp oil 1 large yellow onion, diced 1 poblano, diced 1-2 jalapeños, diced 1 bell pepper, diced Meat (if desired), cook first if using 1-2 tbsp cumin 1-2 tbsp chili powder Pepper to taste Garlic salt to taste 4 cups shredded mexican cheese Salsa, avocado, fresh diced tomatoes, greens Instructions Cook meat/veggies with spices in large frying pan. Add spices near end of cooking to preserve flavor (especially important for cumin and chili powder) With veggies cooked, add half the cheese and mix together well. It will melt quick Set aside and now to make the quesadillas On cutting board, prepare as follows: one tortilla → thin layer of cheese (think few pinches) → some mixture as made → thin layer of cheese again → another tortilla. Do this for each as you go Add a little oil to same pan over medium heat. Cook on each side until browned (do not flip too early or it will fall apart). Also pressing down with spatula helps the cheese stick/melt to tortilla Cook one at a time and cut into 4ths to serve Top with salsa and other stuff
