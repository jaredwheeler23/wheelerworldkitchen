Classic Roast Chicken

Ingredients 1 whole chicken (4-5 lbs) 4 tbsp butter, softened 2 lemons, halved 1 head garlic, halved Fresh herbs (rosemary, thyme) Salt and pepper Instructions Preheat oven to 425°F Pat chicken dry with paper towels Rub butter under and over the skin Season generously with salt and pepper Stuff cavity with lemon halves, garlic, and herbs Tie legs together with kitchen twine Roast for 1.5 hours until golden and internal temp reaches 165°F Rest for 15 minutes before carving

Creamy Mac and Cheese

Ingredients 1 lb elbow macaroni 4 tbsp butter 4 tbsp flour 3 cups milk 3 cups shredded cheddar cheese 1 cup shredded mozzarella Salt, pepper, and paprika 1 cup breadcrumbs 2 tbsp melted butter Instructions Cook macaroni according to package directions, drain In large pot, melt butter and whisk in flour Gradually add milk, whisking constantly Cook until thickened, about 5 minutes Remove from heat, stir in cheeses until melted Add cooked macaroni, season with salt, pepper, and paprika Transfer to greased baking dish Mix breadcrumbs with melted butter, sprinkle on top Bake at 375°F for 25-30 minutes until golden

Fish and Chips

Ingredients 2 lbs cod or haddock fillets 4 large potatoes, cut into thick fries 1 1/2 cups flour 1 tsp baking powder 1 cup cold beer Salt and pepper Vegetable oil for frying Malt vinegar Tartar sauce Instructions Soak potato fries in cold water for 30 minutes Heat oil to 350°F Fry potatoes in batches until golden, drain Mix flour, baking powder, salt, and pepper Whisk in cold beer until smooth Increase oil temp to 375°F Dip fish in batter, fry until golden (4-5 minutes) Drain on paper towels Serve with malt vinegar and tartar sauce

Mushroom Risotto

Ingredients 1.5 cups Arborio rice 8 oz mushrooms, sliced 6 cups chicken or vegetable broth, warmed 1 onion, finely diced 3 cloves garlic, minced 1/2 cup white wine 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese 3 tbsp butter 2 tbsp olive oil Fresh parsley Salt and pepper Instructions Sauté mushrooms in butter until golden, set aside In same pot, heat olive oil and cook onion until soft Add garlic and rice, stir for 2 minutes Add wine and stir until absorbed Add broth one ladle at a time, stirring constantly Continue adding broth until rice is creamy and al dente (about 25 minutes) Stir in mushrooms, Parmesan, and remaining butter Season with salt and pepper, garnish with parsley

Osso Buco

Ingredients 6 veal shanks Flour for dredging 2 carrots, diced 2 celery stalks, diced 1 onion, diced 4 cloves garlic, minced 1 cup white wine 2 cups beef broth 28 oz can crushed tomatoes 2 bay leaves Fresh thyme Salt and pepper Gremolata: lemon zest, parsley, garlic Instructions Season veal shanks, dredge in flour Brown shanks in olive oil, set aside Sauté carrots, celery, and onion until soft Add garlic, cook 1 minute Add wine, simmer until reduced Add broth, tomatoes, bay leaves, and thyme Return shanks to pot, bring to simmer Cover and braise in 325°F oven for 2.5 hours Top with gremolata before serving Serve with risotto

Shepherd's Pie

Ingredients 2 lbs ground lamb (or beef for Cottage Pie) 1 onion, diced 2 carrots, diced 1 cup peas 2 tbsp tomato paste 1 cup beef broth 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce 4 cups mashed potatoes 2 tbsp butter Salt and pepper Fresh thyme Instructions Brown ground lamb in large skillet, drain excess fat Add onion and carrots, cook until softened Stir in tomato paste, broth, Worcestershire, and thyme Add peas, simmer 10 minutes Transfer to baking dish Spread mashed potatoes evenly over top Dot with butter Bake at 400°F for 25-30 minutes until golden

Southern Fried Chicken

Ingredients 3 lbs chicken pieces 2 cups buttermilk 2 cups flour 2 tsp paprika 1 tsp garlic powder 1 tsp onion powder 1 tsp cayenne pepper Salt and pepper Vegetable oil for frying Instructions Marinate chicken in buttermilk for at least 2 hours Mix flour and all spices in large bowl Heat oil to 350°F in deep fryer or large pot Dredge chicken pieces in seasoned flour Fry in batches for 12-15 minutes until golden Drain on paper towels Season with additional salt while hot

Spaghetti Carbonara

Ingredients 1 lb spaghetti 6 oz pancetta or bacon, diced 4 eggs 1 cup Parmesan cheese, grated Black pepper Salt Instructions Cook spaghetti in salted boiling water until al dente While pasta cooks, fry pancetta until crispy Beat eggs with Parmesan and lots of black pepper Reserve 1 cup pasta water, then drain pasta Remove pan from heat, add hot pasta to pancetta Quickly stir in egg mixture, adding pasta water to create creamy sauce Serve immediately with extra Parmesan and pepper
