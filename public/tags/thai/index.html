Pad Thai

Ingredients Veggies (the “meat of the dish”) 8-12 ounces flat rice noodles (quantity depends on how noodle heavy you like it) 3 tbsp olive oil 4 cloves garlic minced 1 large head broccoli, cut into 1 in. pieces or a smidge larger per preference 12 ounces whole mushrooms, leave whole! 1 bell pepper, thinly sliced 1 yellow onion, sliced thin 3 packages of green onions, diced white side plus a little of the green 3 eggs Required mixers after cooking 3/4 cup thai green chili lime almonds from trader joes, chopped smaller (or just use peanuts if no allergy) 1 cup bean sprouts More green onion (optional) chopped same as above 2 limes (squirt all juice in when called for). Or a few tbsp lime juice…tastes the same 1 bunch of cilantro, chopped roughly Sauce 4 tbsp fish sauce 1.5 tbsp soy sauce 6.5 tbsp brown sugar 2.5 tbsp rice vinegar 2 tbsp sriracha hot sauce (or less to taste) 5 tbsp almond butter (or peanut butter if no allergy) or much more to taste Instructions Cook noodles to package directions and set aside. If they get sticky, right before serving you can pour cold water in pan with them, mix with hands to unstick, then drain again. Do not let sit in water though as they will get soggy. Mix sauce ingredients well. Set aside Heat oil in large wok or deep pan. Add broccoli and garlic, cook for a couple minutes. Add rest of veggies and cover, stirring occasionally, until cooked. Push things to side of wok and cook eggs on other side. Scramble them and chop with stirring utensil into bits. Mix back together and add all of topping ingredients plus sauce plus noodles. Serve. Add additional green onions, peanuts/almonds, cilantro, limes wedges as desired (not required if you add plenty when making it). Red Curry Thai Noodle Soup

Ingredients Olive oil for cooking (just a few tbsp at most) 1.5 pounds chicken thighs cut into 0.5" chunks Salt and pepper to taste (for the chicken) 5 cloves garlic minced 1 red or orange bell pepper, diced 1 onion diced 4 tbsp red curry paste (or most of a small bottle) 2 tbsp grated or minced ginger 6 cups chicken broth 1 can coconut milk (full fat) 7 oz rice noodles 2 tbsp fish sauce 3 tsp brown sugar 6 green onions sliced small 1/2 cup chopped cilantro 1/2 cup chopped basil 2.5 tbsp lime juice Instructions In a large pot over med-high heat, heat oil and pan fry the chicken until golden. You don’t have to fully cook. Set aside but leave the drippings in pot Add garlic, pepper, and onion. Cook until tender Ginger and curry paste. Stir for <1 minute until fragrant Add chicken broth, coconut milk, and partly cooked chicken. Simmer for 10 minutes to soak in the flavors and finish cooking the chicken Add noodles, fish sauce and brown sugar. Cook until noodles are tender (~5 minutes or so, check package) Remove from heat and add remaining ingredients and stir well Serve Notes If you want to do bone-in chicken and get much better chicken broth in the process. Add 6 thighs (with salt, pepper, dash of dried basil) to a pressure cooker with 4 cup package of good chicken broth (kitchen basics is the best). Pressure cook high for 30 minutes. Then debone the chicken and add all chicken and broth to pot. This gives it all a richer flavor ...

Thai Chicken Curry with Coconut Milk

Ingredients 3 pounds skinless and boneless chicken thighs 2 - 14 ounce can full-fat coconut milk 5 tablespoons peanut butter (or almond butter) Whole jar Thai red curry paste 2 tablespoon sweet chili sauce optional 3 teaspoons garlic powder 3 teaspoon salt 1 teaspoon ginger powder 1 teaspoon cayenne, adjust to taste Optional Toppings: Handful of fresh Thai basil and/or cilantro chopped Crushed peanuts or almonds 1 lime cut into wedges or just lime juice Instructions Add the coconut milk, chicken, peanut butter, curry paste, chili sauce and spices to an instant pot and mix well Pressure cook at high heat and pressure for 5 minutes then natural release for another 10 minutes. Thai Chicken Noodle Soup

Broth: 1 tbl vegetable oil 1/2 cup onion (or shallots) thinly sliced 1 tbl ginger minced (~1 inch knob) 2 tbl Thai green curry paste 4 cups low sodium chicken broth (kitchen basics is best) 1 can coconut milk 2 tbl fish sauce 4 packed tsp brown sugar 2 tbl lime juice (1 lime) 1/2 tsp turmeric For serving: 4 oz thin rice noodles 2 cups shredded chicken (from a rotisserie chicken ideally) Handful fresh cilantro 3 bunches of green onions, only the white part sliced Siracha Lime wedges Instructions: In a soup pot over medium low heat, cook onion and ginger. Stir frequently until softened. Add curry paste and cook, stirring constantly for ~1 minute more. Add chicken broth, coconut milk, fish sauce, brown sugar, lime juice, and turmeric. Bring to simmer and simmer for 5 minutes. Meanwhile, cook the rice noodles according to package directions. Serve. Divide chicken and noodles in bowls. Ladle in broth. Add as desired: green onions. Cilantro, Siracha and lime wedges.
