Pressure Cooker Chicken Pho Traditional Vietnamese chicken pho with aromatic spices, made faster using a pressure cooker. Add halved onions, ginger, and cook without moving until well charred (about 5 minutes). Take this step seriously, more char is good, make it smoke! Lower heat if smoking excessively. ...

Southern Fried Chicken

Ingredients 3 lbs chicken pieces 2 cups buttermilk 2 cups flour 2 tsp paprika 1 tsp garlic powder 1 tsp onion powder 1 tsp cayenne pepper Salt and pepper Vegetable oil for frying Instructions Marinate chicken in buttermilk for at least 2 hours Mix flour and all spices in large bowl Heat oil to 350°F in deep fryer or large pot Dredge chicken pieces in seasoned flour Fry in batches for 12-15 minutes until golden Drain on paper towels Season with additional salt while hot

Sweet and Sour Chicken

Ingredients 2 lbs chicken breast, cubed 1 cup flour 2 eggs, beaten 1 bell pepper, cubed 1 onion, cubed 1 cup pineapple chunks 3/4 cup sugar 1/2 cup ketchup 1/3 cup vinegar 2 tbsp soy sauce 1 tbsp cornstarch Vegetable oil Instructions Mix sauce ingredients (sugar through cornstarch) Dip chicken in egg, then flour Deep fry chicken until golden and crispy In separate pan, stir-fry peppers and onions Add pineapple and sauce, simmer until thickened Add fried chicken, toss to coat Serve over rice
