Butter Chicken

Ingredients 2 lbs chicken thighs, cubed 1 cup yogurt 2 tbsp garam masala 1 tbsp turmeric 1 onion, diced 4 cloves garlic, minced 1 tbsp ginger, minced 28 oz can crushed tomatoes 1 cup heavy cream 4 tbsp butter Fresh cilantro Salt Instructions Marinate chicken in yogurt, garam masala, and turmeric for 30 minutes Sauté onion, garlic, and ginger in butter Add marinated chicken, cook until browned Add crushed tomatoes, simmer 20 minutes Stir in heavy cream Simmer 10 more minutes Garnish with cilantro Serve with naan or rice

Chicken Burritos

Ingredients 2 lbs chicken breast, diced 2 cups cooked rice 1 can black beans, drained 1 bell pepper, diced 1 onion, diced 2 tsp cumin 1 tsp chili powder 6 large flour tortillas Cheese, sour cream, guacamole, salsa Instructions Cook chicken in large skillet until golden Add bell pepper and onion, cook until softened Stir in rice, beans, cumin, and chili powder Warm tortillas in microwave Divide filling among tortillas Add cheese and desired toppings Fold in sides and roll up tightly Optional: Grill seam-side down until golden

Chicken Fried Rice

Ingredients 4 cups cooked rice (day-old works best) 2 chicken breasts, diced 3 eggs, beaten 1 cup mixed vegetables (peas, carrots, corn) 3 green onions, sliced 3 cloves garlic, minced 1 tbsp ginger, minced 3 tbsp soy sauce 2 tsp sesame oil Vegetable oil Instructions Heat wok over high heat with oil Scramble eggs, set aside Stir-fry chicken until cooked through, set aside Add vegetables, garlic, and ginger, stir-fry 2 minutes Add rice, breaking up clumps Add soy sauce and sesame oil Return chicken and eggs to wok, toss to combine Top with green onions

Chicken Parmesan

Ingredients 4 chicken breasts, pounded thin 2 cups breadcrumbs 1 cup Parmesan cheese, grated 2 eggs, beaten 1 cup flour 2 cups marinara sauce 2 cups mozzarella cheese, shredded Fresh basil Salt and pepper Olive oil for frying Instructions Set up breading station: flour, beaten eggs, breadcrumbs mixed with Parmesan Season chicken with salt and pepper Dredge in flour, dip in egg, coat with breadcrumb mixture Fry in olive oil until golden on both sides Place chicken in baking dish, top with marinara and mozzarella Bake at 400°F for 15-20 minutes until cheese is melted Garnish with fresh basil Serve over pasta

Chicken Teriyaki

Ingredients 2 lbs chicken thighs 1/2 cup soy sauce 1/4 cup mirin 1/4 cup sake or white wine 3 tbsp sugar 2 cloves garlic, minced 1 tbsp ginger, minced 1 tbsp cornstarch Sesame seeds Green onions Instructions Mix soy sauce, mirin, sake, sugar, garlic, and ginger Marinate chicken for 15 minutes Remove chicken, reserve marinade Grill or pan-fry chicken until cooked through Simmer reserved marinade with cornstarch until thickened Brush glaze over cooked chicken Garnish with sesame seeds and green onions Serve over rice

Classic Roast Chicken

Ingredients 1 whole chicken (4-5 lbs) 4 tbsp butter, softened 2 lemons, halved 1 head garlic, halved Fresh herbs (rosemary, thyme) Salt and pepper Instructions Preheat oven to 425°F Pat chicken dry with paper towels Rub butter under and over the skin Season generously with salt and pepper Stuff cavity with lemon halves, garlic, and herbs Tie legs together with kitchen twine Roast for 1.5 hours until golden and internal temp reaches 165°F Rest for 15 minutes before carving

Coq au Vin

Ingredients 3 lbs chicken pieces 6 oz bacon, diced 8 oz mushrooms, quartered 12 pearl onions, peeled 3 cloves garlic, minced 2 carrots, sliced 2 cups red wine 1 cup chicken broth 2 tbsp tomato paste 3 tbsp flour 3 tbsp butter Fresh thyme and bay leaf Salt and pepper Instructions Brown bacon in Dutch oven, set aside Season chicken, brown in bacon fat, set aside Sauté mushrooms and onions, set aside Add carrots and garlic to pot, cook 2 minutes Stir in flour, then add wine, broth, and tomato paste Add thyme and bay leaf Return chicken and bacon to pot Simmer covered for 1.5 hours Add mushrooms and onions, cook 15 more minutes Serve with crusty bread or mashed potatoes
