Beef and Broccoli

Ingredients 1.5 lbs flank steak, sliced thin 1 lb broccoli florets 4 cloves garlic, minced 1 tbsp ginger, minced 1/4 cup soy sauce 2 tbsp oyster sauce 1 tbsp cornstarch 1 tbsp brown sugar 1/2 cup beef broth Sesame oil Vegetable oil Instructions Mix soy sauce, oyster sauce, cornstarch, sugar, and broth Marinate beef in half the sauce for 15 minutes Blanch broccoli in boiling water for 2 minutes, drain Heat wok with oil over high heat Stir-fry beef until browned, set aside Add garlic and ginger, cook 30 seconds Add broccoli and remaining sauce Return beef to wok, toss until sauce thickens Drizzle with sesame oil Serve over rice

Beef Wellington

Ingredients 2 lbs beef tenderloin 1 lb mushrooms, finely chopped 2 shallots, minced 3 cloves garlic, minced 8 oz pâté or liver mousse 8 slices prosciutto 1 sheet puff pastry 2 egg yolks, beaten Fresh thyme Salt and pepper Instructions Sear beef on all sides, season with salt and pepper, cool Sauté mushrooms, shallots, and garlic until dry (duxelles) Season duxelles with thyme, salt, and pepper Lay out prosciutto on plastic wrap, overlapping Spread pâté over prosciutto Spread duxelles over pâté Place beef on top, roll tightly in plastic, chill 30 minutes Roll out puff pastry, wrap around beef Brush with egg wash Bake at 425°F for 35-40 minutes until pastry is golden Rest 10 minutes before slicing
