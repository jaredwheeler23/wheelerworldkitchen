Creamy Mac and Cheese

Ingredients 1 lb elbow macaroni 4 tbsp butter 4 tbsp flour 3 cups milk 3 cups shredded cheddar cheese 1 cup shredded mozzarella Salt, pepper, and paprika 1 cup breadcrumbs 2 tbsp melted butter Instructions Cook macaroni according to package directions, drain In large pot, melt butter and whisk in flour Gradually add milk, whisking constantly Cook until thickened, about 5 minutes Remove from heat, stir in cheeses until melted Add cooked macaroni, season with salt, pepper, and paprika Transfer to greased baking dish Mix breadcrumbs with melted butter, sprinkle on top Bake at 375°F for 25-30 minutes until golden

Penne Arrabbiata

Ingredients 1 lb penne pasta 3 tbsp olive oil 6 cloves garlic, minced 1 tsp red pepper flakes (adjust to taste) 28 oz can crushed tomatoes 1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped Salt Parmesan cheese for serving Instructions Cook penne in salted boiling water until al dente Heat olive oil in large skillet Sauté garlic and red pepper flakes until fragrant Add crushed tomatoes and salt Simmer for 15 minutes Toss cooked pasta with sauce Garnish with parsley and Parmesan

Spaghetti Carbonara

Ingredients 1 lb spaghetti 6 oz pancetta or bacon, diced 4 eggs 1 cup Parmesan cheese, grated Black pepper Salt Instructions Cook spaghetti in salted boiling water until al dente While pasta cooks, fry pancetta until crispy Beat eggs with Parmesan and lots of black pepper Reserve 1 cup pasta water, then drain pasta Remove pan from heat, add hot pasta to pancetta Quickly stir in egg mixture, adding pasta water to create creamy sauce Serve immediately with extra Parmesan and pepper
