Fresh Tomato Fettuccine with Garlic

Ingredients 1 lb fettuccine, cooked according to package directions 6 large ripe tomatoes peeled, seeded and diced 1 large cup, packed, fresh basil, chopped 1/2 cup parsley chopped ¼ cup olive oil 6 cloves of garlic minced 1 jalapeno minced and seeded Instructions Peel the tomatoes by putting in boiling water for 15 seconds, remove and cut slit in skin, and it should peel right off. Combine tomatoes, basil and parsley in a bowl. Heat oil and saute garlic and pepper until soft. Add tomato mixture to hot pan for another minute to warm up. Mix with pasta well and serve (it is best to have pasta warm and just drained prior to mixing so things come out fully warm)

Green Bean Salad

Ingredients 1 lb or 4 cups fresh green beans (stem on) 1/2 head romaine lettuce (2 cups) 1 red onion, sliced thin 1 cup feta crumbles 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes, halved 1/4 cup chopped walnuts 1 tbsp fresh oregano, chopped 2 tsp red wine vinegar 1 clove garlic, minced 2 tbsp olive oil Instructions Cook beans (stem on) until crisp. Leave stems on but cut off thin, wooden parts as desired Mix ingredients and dressing separate. Then combine in a large serving bowl!

Homemade Pasta Dough

Ingredients 2 cups all purpose flour 3 large eggs 1/2 tsp salt 1/2 tbsp olive oil Instructions Place flour on clean, flat surface and make a nest. Add eggs, oil and salt to center and use fork to break/mix things in while keeping flour walls intact Now use hands to gently bring the flour inward to incorporate Work ball with your hands to bring it together and continue to knead for 10 minutes or so. (it will feel dry at first but be patient). It should beome conhesive and smooth. If still dry and kneaded to death, add a smidge of water to finalize it. IF too sticky, add a smidge of flour Let dough rest at room temp for 30 minutes Now, proceed to use dough such as with pasta attachment on kitchen aid or something

Homemade Salsa

Ingredients 1.25 lbs roma tomatoes (5-6) 1 (14.5 oz) can petite diced tomatoes (low salt and maybe fire roasted) 3 green onions, ends trimmed 1/4 red onion 1 jalapeño (up to you on seeded, usually not) Batch cilantro 1 large clove garlic 2 tbsp lime juice 1/2 tsp chili powder 1/4 tsp ground cumin 1 tsp salt or little less Salt and pepper to taste Instructions Combine all ingredients in a food processor (in batches if necessary) and combine. If you have time, allow salsa to refrigerate for a few hours before serving to better blend spices (it is worth it)
