Chinese Cashew Chicken

Ingredients 2 lb chicken thighs cut into chunks 4 tsp soy sauce 4 tbsp oil 2 diced jalapenos seeded 6 cloves or more garlic minced (more is better here) 1 onion diced 3-4 bell peppers diced 1.5 cups cashews (or almonds with allergy) 1 handful green onions (cut into 1.5 inch shoots) Sauce 2 tbsp oyster sauce 2 tbsp soy sauce 2 tsp golden mountain sauce (or maggi sauce (or just more fish sauce)) 2 tsp fish sauce 3 tsp sugar 3 tbsp thai chili paste 2 tsp toasted sesame oil (or forget the toasted part) Instructions With cut chicken, marinade in 4 tsp soy sauce for 15 minutes. Meanwhile roast the cashews at 350 F for ~10 minutes until they start to darken. Do not burn them. Also meanwhile, combine all sauce ingredients in a bowl to add later. Heat oil in large wok and cook on high heat the chicken to nicely brown and cook all the way through (or at least mostly so, it can finish in the next step if needed). Add all veggies and cook until tender. Add sauce and cook another couple minutes. Serve with cashews and rice. (note: cashews become soggy if left in dish, serve at table and don’t dump them all in the dish)

Pad Thai

Ingredients Veggies (the “meat of the dish”) 8-12 ounces flat rice noodles (quantity depends on how noodle heavy you like it) 3 tbsp olive oil 4 cloves garlic minced 1 large head broccoli, cut into 1 in. pieces or a smidge larger per preference 12 ounces whole mushrooms, leave whole! 1 bell pepper, thinly sliced 1 yellow onion, sliced thin 3 packages of green onions, diced white side plus a little of the green 3 eggs Required mixers after cooking 3/4 cup thai green chili lime almonds from trader joes, chopped smaller (or just use peanuts if no allergy) 1 cup bean sprouts More green onion (optional) chopped same as above 2 limes (squirt all juice in when called for). Or a few tbsp lime juice…tastes the same 1 bunch of cilantro, chopped roughly Sauce 4 tbsp fish sauce 1.5 tbsp soy sauce 6.5 tbsp brown sugar 2.5 tbsp rice vinegar 2 tbsp sriracha hot sauce (or less to taste) 5 tbsp almond butter (or peanut butter if no allergy) or much more to taste Instructions Cook noodles to package directions and set aside. If they get sticky, right before serving you can pour cold water in pan with them, mix with hands to unstick, then drain again. Do not let sit in water though as they will get soggy. Mix sauce ingredients well. Set aside Heat oil in large wok or deep pan. Add broccoli and garlic, cook for a couple minutes. Add rest of veggies and cover, stirring occasionally, until cooked. Push things to side of wok and cook eggs on other side. Scramble them and chop with stirring utensil into bits. Mix back together and add all of topping ingredients plus sauce plus noodles. Serve. Add additional green onions, peanuts/almonds, cilantro, limes wedges as desired (not required if you add plenty when making it). Pressure Cooker Chicken Pho

Ingredients Broth: 2 tbl vegetable oil 1 large onion, cut in half 1 small hand ginger (few inches at least), roughly sliced 1 bunch cilantro 3 star anise pods 1 cinnamon stick 4 cloves 1 tsp fennel seeds 1 tsp coriander seeds 8 cups of water 6-8 chicken drumsticks (or thighs) 1/4 cup fish sauce 2 tbl rock sugar For serving: 4 servings pho noodles prepared according to package directions 1 small red onion thinly sliced 1/2 cup thinly sliced green onions (white only) 2 cups mixed herbs Cilantro, basil mint 2 cups bean sprouts 1 jalapeño sliced Lime wedges Hoisin sauce and Siracha Instructions Heat oil in pressure cooker (on sauté mode) on high heat until smoking. Add halved onions, ginger, and cook without moving until well charred (~5 minutes). Take this step seriously, more char is good, make it smoke! Lower heat if smoking excessively. Add rest of broth ingredients to pot. Seal pressure cooker and set for high pressure and heat for 30 minutes. Note: time does not include time it takes to get to pressure/temperature. When done, release pressure valve (for electric pressure cookers) and wait until safe to open. Cook noodles according to package directions and set aside. Get ready other serving ingredients and set aside. After opening pressure cooker, remove chicken to cutting board and debone it. Rest of broth should be put through a fine mesh strainer into a clean pot. Discard anything caught in the strainer. To serve: split noodles, chicken, sliced onion, green onion into bowls. Pour broth into bowls. Serve and individuals add herbs, sprouts, peppers, lime, and sauces as desired.
