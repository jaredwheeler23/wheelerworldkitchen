Marshmallow

Rice Crispy Treats

Ingredients 1/4 cup butter 4 cups miniature marshmallows 5 cups crispy rice cereal Instructions Melt butter in a large saucepan over low heat Add marshmallows and stir until melted and well combined. Cook 2 minutes longer, stirring constantly. Remove from heat Stir in crispy rice cereal until well coated Press mixture evenly and firmly into a buttered 9x13-inch pan using a buttered spatula or waxed paper Cut into 2-inch squares when cool Notes Change out cereal for others like chocolate rice crispies, captain crunch, etc. Can add mini chocolate chips or m&ms too
