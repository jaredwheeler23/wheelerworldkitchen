Beef Wellington

Ingredients 2 lbs beef tenderloin 1 lb mushrooms, finely chopped 2 shallots, minced 3 cloves garlic, minced 8 oz pâté or liver mousse 8 slices prosciutto 1 sheet puff pastry 2 egg yolks, beaten Fresh thyme Salt and pepper Instructions Sear beef on all sides, season with salt and pepper, cool Sauté mushrooms, shallots, and garlic until dry (duxelles) Season duxelles with thyme, salt, and pepper Lay out prosciutto on plastic wrap, overlapping Spread pâté over prosciutto Spread duxelles over pâté Place beef on top, roll tightly in plastic, chill 30 minutes Roll out puff pastry, wrap around beef Brush with egg wash Bake at 425°F for 35-40 minutes until pastry is golden Rest 10 minutes before slicing

Coq au Vin

Ingredients 3 lbs chicken pieces 6 oz bacon, diced 8 oz mushrooms, quartered 12 pearl onions, peeled 3 cloves garlic, minced 2 carrots, sliced 2 cups red wine 1 cup chicken broth 2 tbsp tomato paste 3 tbsp flour 3 tbsp butter Fresh thyme and bay leaf Salt and pepper Instructions Brown bacon in Dutch oven, set aside Season chicken, brown in bacon fat, set aside Sauté mushrooms and onions, set aside Add carrots and garlic to pot, cook 2 minutes Stir in flour, then add wine, broth, and tomato paste Add thyme and bay leaf Return chicken and bacon to pot Simmer covered for 1.5 hours Add mushrooms and onions, cook 15 more minutes Serve with crusty bread or mashed potatoes

Fish and Chips

Ingredients 2 lbs cod or haddock fillets 4 large potatoes, cut into thick fries 1 1/2 cups flour 1 tsp baking powder 1 cup cold beer Salt and pepper Vegetable oil for frying Malt vinegar Tartar sauce Instructions Soak potato fries in cold water for 30 minutes Heat oil to 350°F Fry potatoes in batches until golden, drain Mix flour, baking powder, salt, and pepper Whisk in cold beer until smooth Increase oil temp to 375°F Dip fish in batter, fry until golden (4-5 minutes) Drain on paper towels Serve with malt vinegar and tartar sauce
