Beef Fajitas

Ingredients 1.5 lbs flank steak, sliced thin 3 bell peppers, sliced 1 large onion, sliced 3 tbsp lime juice 2 tsp cumin 2 tsp chili powder 1 tsp paprika Salt and pepper 8 flour tortillas Sour cream, guacamole, salsa Instructions Marinate beef in lime juice and spices for 30 minutes Heat oil in large skillet over high heat Cook beef in batches until browned, set aside Add peppers and onions, cook until tender-crisp Return beef to skillet, toss to combine Warm tortillas Serve beef mixture in tortillas with desired toppings

Carnitas

Ingredients 4 lbs pork shoulder, cut into chunks 1 onion, quartered 4 cloves garlic, minced 2 oranges, juiced 2 limes, juiced 2 tsp cumin 1 tsp oregano Salt and pepper Corn tortillas Fresh cilantro, diced onion, lime wedges Instructions Place pork in slow cooker Add onion, garlic, citrus juices, and spices Cook on low for 6 hours until very tender Remove pork and shred with forks Broil shredded pork for 5-10 minutes until crispy edges form Serve in warm corn tortillas with cilantro, onion, and lime

Chicken Burritos

Ingredients 2 lbs chicken breast, diced 2 cups cooked rice 1 can black beans, drained 1 bell pepper, diced 1 onion, diced 2 tsp cumin 1 tsp chili powder 6 large flour tortillas Cheese, sour cream, guacamole, salsa Instructions Cook chicken in large skillet until golden Add bell pepper and onion, cook until softened Stir in rice, beans, cumin, and chili powder Warm tortillas in microwave Divide filling among tortillas Add cheese and desired toppings Fold in sides and roll up tightly Optional: Grill seam-side down until golden

Chile Verde

Ingredients 3 lbs pork shoulder, cubed 2 lbs tomatillos, husked and halved 2 poblano peppers, roasted and peeled 2 jalapeños 1 onion, quartered 4 cloves garlic 1 cup chicken broth 1 tsp cumin Fresh cilantro Salt and pepper Instructions Roast tomatillos and peppers under broiler until charred Blend roasted vegetables with onion, garlic, and broth Brown pork in batches in Dutch oven Add blended sauce and cumin Simmer covered for 4 hours until pork is tender Season with salt and pepper Garnish with fresh cilantro Serve with rice or in tortillas

Classic Beef Tacos

Ingredients 2 lbs ground beef 1 onion, diced 3 cloves garlic, minced 2 tbsp chili powder 1 tsp cumin 1 tsp paprika Salt and pepper 12 taco shells Toppings: lettuce, tomatoes, cheese, sour cream, salsa Instructions Brown ground beef in large skillet over medium-high heat Add onion and garlic, cook until softened Drain excess fat Add spices and 1/2 cup water Simmer for 10 minutes until mixture thickens Warm taco shells according to package directions Fill shells with beef and desired toppings
