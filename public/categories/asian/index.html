Asian Noodle Bowl (Not Soup)

Ingredients Sauce: 1/4 cup soy sauce 2 tsp grated lime zest 1/4 cup fresh lime juice 1/4 cup brown sugar 1/2 tsp minced jalapeño (or more), or chinese chili paste 2 tsp crushed or minced garlic Toppings: 1/4 cup chopped basil 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro 1 cup roasted peanuts or almonds Main: 1/4 cup vegetable oil 2 cups sliced scallions (cut into 1" pieces) 6 cups thinly sliced broccoli 2 cups chopped fresh tomatoes 2 tbsp grated ginger 8 oz rice stick noodles 1 tbsp dark sesame oil 4 large eggs, beaten Instructions Make the sauce, in a bowl whisk together sauce ingredients. Set sauce on the table Bring water to boil and cook noodles according to package directions Heat oil in wok or large skillet and saute scallions until soft. Stir in broccoli and cook for a few minutes until color deepens. Stir in the tomatoes and ginger and cover for about 7 minutes, or at least until broccoli is tender At same time, heat dark sesame oil in a skillet, pour in the beaten eggs and cook until the edges are set and then flip to cook the opposite side. Remove the eggs from the heat and slice into thin strips Serve each person a large bowl filled with a mix of noodles, topping and strips of fried egg. They, at the table, top with sauce, fresh herbs, and peanuts/almonds

Fried Rice

Ingredients 3 tablespoons butter, divided 3 large eggs, whisked 2 large carrots, peeled and diced 1 large white onion, diced 1/2 cup frozen peas 4 cloves garlic, minced salt and black pepper to taste 4 cups cooked rice (I prefer short-grain white rice) 5 green onions, thinly sliced (or just one bunch) 4 tablespoons soy sauce, or more to taste 2 teaspoons oyster sauce (optional) 1/2 teaspoons toasted sesame oil (if you have it but not required) Instructions Heat 1/2 tablespoon of butter in a large sauté pan over medium-high heat until melted. Add egg, and cook until scrambled, stirring occasionally. Remove egg, and transfer to a separate plate Add an additional 1 tablespoon butter to the pan and heat until melted. Add carrots, onion, peas and garlic, and season with a generous pinch of salt and pepper. Sauté for about 5 minutes or until the onion and carrots are soft Increase heat to high, add in the remaining 1 1/2 tablespoons of butter, and stir until melted. Immediately add the rice, green onions, soy sauce and oyster sauce (if using), and stir until combined Continue sautéing for an additional 3 minutes to fry the rice, stirring occasionally. (let the rice rest for a bit between stirs so that it can crisp up on the bottom.) This can take longer depending on heat used and make sure to get crispy to preference but not burned Then add in the eggs and stir to combine. Remove from heat, and stir in the sesame oil until combined. Taste and season with extra soy sauce, if needed Serve immediately

Miso, Noodle, Peanut Soup

Ingredients Broth: 2 cm fresh ginger, finely grated 2 green onions, minced 1.5 cups water 2 tbsp miso paste (either white or red, dealers choice) 2 tbsp peanut butter no sugar added (or almond or similar, get unsweetened if possible) Noodles: 1 packet ramen, or equivalent in rice noodles, or soba or udon or whatever available For serving: 1 sheet nori (seaweed) 1 red chili, finely sliced (or red chili flakes work fine) 2 tbsp fresh coriander (or 1-2 tbsp ground coriander works) 2 tsp black or white sesame seeds Instructions Cook the noodles separate as per package. Then place in serving bowl Place ginger and onion in small sauce pan with tiny bit of oil. Turn on the heat and just start to cook/sizzle, then add the miso and peanut butter along with the water. Whisk together and slowly bring to simmer. The instant it starts simmering, take off the heat and pour in serving bowl with the noodles Add the servings ingredients to the bowl and serve immediately

Pressure Cooker Chicken Pho

Ingredients Broth: 2 tbl vegetable oil 1 large onion, cut in half 1 small hand ginger (few inches at least), roughly sliced 1 bunch cilantro 3 star anise pods 1 cinnamon stick 4 cloves 1 tsp fennel seeds 1 tsp coriander seeds 8 cups of water 6-8 chicken drumsticks (or thighs) 1/4 cup fish sauce 2 tbl rock sugar For serving: 4 servings pho noodles prepared according to package directions 1 small red onion thinly sliced 1/2 cup thinly sliced green onions (white only) 2 cups mixed herbs Cilantro, basil mint 2 cups bean sprouts 1 jalapeño sliced Lime wedges Hoisin sauce and Siracha Instructions Heat oil in pressure cooker (on sauté mode) on high heat until smoking. Add halved onions, ginger, and cook without moving until well charred (~5 minutes). Take this step seriously, more char is good, make it smoke! Lower heat if smoking excessively. Add rest of broth ingredients to pot. Seal pressure cooker and set for high pressure and heat for 30 minutes. Note: time does not include time it takes to get to pressure/temperature. When done, release pressure valve (for electric pressure cookers) and wait until safe to open. Cook noodles according to package directions and set aside. Get ready other serving ingredients and set aside. After opening pressure cooker, remove chicken to cutting board and debone it. Rest of broth should be put through a fine mesh strainer into a clean pot. Discard anything caught in the strainer. To serve: split noodles, chicken, sliced onion, green onion into bowls. Pour broth into bowls. Serve and individuals add herbs, sprouts, peppers, lime, and sauces as desired.

Ramen Variety 2, Not Spicy

Ingredients 4 chicken thighs (or two breasts but thighs are better) Salt and pepper 1 tbsp butter 2 tsp vegetable oil 2 inches fresh ginger, minced 2 cloves garlic, minced 3 tbsp soy sauce 2 tbsp mirin 4 cups good chicken stock (kitchen basics) Salt to taste 2 packs dried ramen noodles (or equivalent in rice noodles) Veggies (at least 2-3 cups of following, your choice): spinach, green onions thinly chopped, jalapeño, peeled carrot (with a potato peeler), can sweet corn (this is really good in this), diced sweet peppers (the kind that don’t need to be cooked), thinly sliced red onion Instructions With butter in medium sized soup pot, cook chicken on both sides (few minutes each) until golden. Make sure to season generously with salt and pepper. Then transfer to sheet and cook in oven another 15 minutes or so until well cooked. Slice or shred and set aside In same pot on medium heat, heat oil and saute garlic and ginger for a few minutes until soft. Add soy sauce and mirin and cook 1 more minute. Add broth and simmer for 15 minutes Cook noodles separately according to package directions Veggies: If any of them need it, saute briefly with sesame seed oil and rice vinegar. This does add a nice flavor and definitely do this if making vegetarian Assemble bowls with mix of veggies, noodles, and chicken. Pour boiling broth on it all and serve. Top with jalapeño at table as desired Notes To make vegetarian, skip the chicken and use very good veggie broth instead of chicken. In this case make sure you saute briefly any veggies with the sesame seed oil and rice vinegar (not much of each and just enough to get the flavor). This adds a nice richness in flavor Cook the noodles separate and not in the broth to conserve water of broth and stretch this to two servings
