Beef and Broccoli

Ingredients 1.5 lbs flank steak, sliced thin 1 lb broccoli florets 4 cloves garlic, minced 1 tbsp ginger, minced 1/4 cup soy sauce 2 tbsp oyster sauce 1 tbsp cornstarch 1 tbsp brown sugar 1/2 cup beef broth Sesame oil Vegetable oil Instructions Mix soy sauce, oyster sauce, cornstarch, sugar, and broth Marinate beef in half the sauce for 15 minutes Blanch broccoli in boiling water for 2 minutes, drain Heat wok with oil over high heat Stir-fry beef until browned, set aside Add garlic and ginger, cook 30 seconds Add broccoli and remaining sauce Return beef to wok, toss until sauce thickens Drizzle with sesame oil Serve over rice

Chicken Fried Rice

Ingredients 4 cups cooked rice (day-old works best) 2 chicken breasts, diced 3 eggs, beaten 1 cup mixed vegetables (peas, carrots, corn) 3 green onions, sliced 3 cloves garlic, minced 1 tbsp ginger, minced 3 tbsp soy sauce 2 tsp sesame oil Vegetable oil Instructions Heat wok over high heat with oil Scramble eggs, set aside Stir-fry chicken until cooked through, set aside Add vegetables, garlic, and ginger, stir-fry 2 minutes Add rice, breaking up clumps Add soy sauce and sesame oil Return chicken and eggs to wok, toss to combine Top with green onions

Chicken Teriyaki

Ingredients 2 lbs chicken thighs 1/2 cup soy sauce 1/4 cup mirin 1/4 cup sake or white wine 3 tbsp sugar 2 cloves garlic, minced 1 tbsp ginger, minced 1 tbsp cornstarch Sesame seeds Green onions Instructions Mix soy sauce, mirin, sake, sugar, garlic, and ginger Marinate chicken for 15 minutes Remove chicken, reserve marinade Grill or pan-fry chicken until cooked through Simmer reserved marinade with cornstarch until thickened Brush glaze over cooked chicken Garnish with sesame seeds and green onions Serve over rice

Korean Bulgogi

Ingredients 2 lbs ribeye or sirloin, thinly sliced 1/2 cup soy sauce 1/4 cup brown sugar 2 tbsp sesame oil 6 cloves garlic, minced 1 tbsp ginger, grated 2 green onions, chopped 1 Asian pear, grated (or apple) 1 tbsp sesame seeds Black pepper Instructions Mix all marinade ingredients (soy sauce through pear) Add beef slices, massage marinade in Refrigerate for at least 2 hours or overnight Heat grill or skillet to high heat Cook beef in batches, 2-3 minutes per side Don’t overcrowd the pan Garnish with sesame seeds and green onions Serve with rice and kimchi

Pad Thai

Ingredients 8 oz rice noodles 1 lb shrimp, peeled 3 eggs 3 tbsp tamarind paste 2 tbsp fish sauce 2 tbsp sugar 3 cloves garlic, minced Bean sprouts Green onions, chopped Peanuts, crushed Lime wedges Vegetable oil Instructions Soak rice noodles in warm water for 30 minutes, drain Mix tamarind paste, fish sauce, and sugar for sauce Heat oil in wok, scramble eggs and set aside Stir-fry shrimp until pink, set aside Add garlic to wok, then add noodles Pour sauce over noodles, toss to coat Add shrimp and eggs back to wok Top with bean sprouts, green onions, and peanuts Serve with lime wedges

Ingredients 2 lbs chicken breast, cubed 1 cup flour 2 eggs, beaten 1 bell pepper, cubed 1 onion, cubed 1 cup pineapple chunks 3/4 cup sugar 1/2 cup ketchup 1/3 cup vinegar 2 tbsp soy sauce 1 tbsp cornstarch Vegetable oil Instructions Mix sauce ingredients (sugar through cornstarch) Dip chicken in egg, then flour Deep fry chicken until golden and crispy In separate pan, stir-fry peppers and onions Add pineapple and sauce, simmer until thickened Add fried chicken, toss to coat Serve over rice
