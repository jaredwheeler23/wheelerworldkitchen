Apple Pie

Ingredients Crust: 2 1/2 cups flour 1 cup cold butter, cubed 1 tsp salt 1 tbsp sugar 6-8 tbsp ice water Filling: 6-8 apples, peeled and sliced 3/4 cup sugar 2 tbsp flour 1 tsp cinnamon 1/4 tsp nutmeg 2 tbsp butter 1 egg for egg wash Instructions Make crust: Mix flour, salt, sugar. Cut in butter until crumbly Add ice water until dough forms. Divide in half, chill 30 minutes Mix apples with sugar, flour, and spices Roll out half the dough, place in 9-inch pie pan Add apple filling, dot with butter Roll out remaining dough for top crust Seal edges, cut vents in top Brush with egg wash, sprinkle with sugar Bake at 425°F for 15 minutes, then 350°F for 45 minutes Cool before slicing

Chocolate Chip Brownies

Ingredients 1 cup butter, melted 2 cups sugar 4 eggs 1 1/2 tsp vanilla 2/3 cup cocoa powder 1 cup flour 1/2 tsp salt 1/2 tsp baking powder 1 1/2 cups chocolate chips Instructions Preheat oven to 350°F, grease 9x13 pan Mix melted butter and sugar Beat in eggs and vanilla Combine cocoa, flour, salt, and baking powder Stir dry ingredients into wet ingredients Fold in chocolate chips Pour into prepared pan Bake 25-30 minutes (don’t overbake for fudgy brownies) Cool completely before cutting

Chocolate Lava Cakes

Ingredients 6 oz dark chocolate, chopped 1/2 cup butter 2 eggs 2 egg yolks 1/4 cup sugar 2 tbsp flour Pinch of salt Butter and cocoa for ramekins Vanilla ice cream for serving Instructions Butter and cocoa dust 4 ramekins Melt chocolate and butter together Whisk eggs, egg yolks, and sugar until thick Fold in melted chocolate Gently fold in flour and salt Divide among ramekins Bake at 425°F for 12-14 minutes Centers should be soft Let stand 1 minute, then invert onto plates Serve immediately with vanilla ice cream

Classic Tiramisu

Ingredients 6 egg yolks 3/4 cup sugar 1 1/3 cups mascarpone cheese 2 cups heavy cream 2 cups strong espresso, cooled 3 tbsp coffee liqueur (optional) 40 ladyfinger cookies Cocoa powder for dusting Dark chocolate shavings (optional) Instructions Whisk egg yolks and sugar until thick and pale Add mascarpone, beat until smooth Whip heavy cream to stiff peaks, fold into mascarpone mixture Mix espresso with coffee liqueur Quickly dip ladyfingers in espresso mixture Layer dipped ladyfingers in 9x13 dish Spread half the mascarpone cream over ladyfingers Repeat with another layer Dust generously with cocoa powder Refrigerate at least 4 hours or overnight

Crème Brûlée

Ingredients 2 cups heavy cream 1 vanilla bean (or 2 tsp vanilla extract) 5 egg yolks 1/2 cup sugar (plus extra for topping) Pinch of salt Instructions Preheat oven to 325°F Heat cream with vanilla bean (split and scraped) until simmering Whisk egg yolks with sugar and salt Slowly pour hot cream into egg mixture, whisking constantly Strain through fine mesh sieve Pour into 6 ramekins Place ramekins in roasting pan, add hot water halfway up sides Bake 40-45 minutes until set but still jiggly Refrigerate at least 3 hours Before serving, sprinkle sugar on top and caramelize with torch

New York Cheesecake

Ingredients Crust: 2 cups graham cracker crumbs 1/2 cup melted butter 1/4 cup sugar Filling: 32 oz cream cheese, softened 1 1/4 cups sugar 4 eggs 1 cup sour cream 2 tsp vanilla extract 1/4 cup flour Instructions Mix graham cracker crumbs, melted butter, and sugar Press into bottom of 9-inch springform pan Bake crust at 325°F for 10 minutes Beat cream cheese until fluffy Add sugar, beat until smooth Add eggs one at a time Mix in sour cream, vanilla, and flour Pour over crust Bake at 325°F for 55-60 minutes (center should jiggle slightly) Turn off oven, leave cheesecake inside for 1 hour Refrigerate at least 4 hours before serving
