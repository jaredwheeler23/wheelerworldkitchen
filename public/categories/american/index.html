Autumn Pot Pie (Christmas Veggie Pot Pie)

Autumn Pot Pie (Christmas Veggie Pot Pie) A stunning vegetarian pot pie perfect for the holidays, featuring butternut squash and a homemade cheddar crust. Ingredients Cheddar Pastry 2 cups all purpose white flour 1/2 tsp salt 1/2 cup cold butter, diced 1 cup chilled, grated sharp cheddar cheese (buy a bag of shredded cheese) 4-5 tbsp ice water Vegetable Filling 1 tbsp vegetable oil 4 cups chopped leeks (use only white and light green parts of 2 medium leeks) 2 bay leaves 1 tsp dried thyme 1 tsp dried sage (or 1 tbsp fresh) 1/2 tsp salt 2 cups green beans (cut into 1 in. lengths) 3/4 cup vegetable stock 3 cups chopped green cabbage 3 cups cubed butternut squash 3 cups sliced mushrooms Cheese Sauce 3 tbsp butter 1/4 cup all purpose white flour 1.5 cups warmed vegetable stock 1/2 tsp dijon mustard or similar 1/2 cup grated cheese, parmesan works Salt and pepper to taste Instructions Start with cheddar pastry. Use a food processor and briefly pulse the flour, salt, and butter until the mixture has lumps about the size of peas. Add cheddar and pulse a few times until the mixture resembles a coarse meal. Add the water, a tbsp at a time, pulsing briefly to incorporate. Press the dough into a ball. Wrap in waxed paper or plastic wrap and refrigerate until ready to roll out. ...

Ingredients 4 lbs pork shoulder 2 tbsp brown sugar 2 tbsp paprika 1 tbsp garlic powder 1 tbsp onion powder 1 tsp cayenne pepper Salt and pepper to taste 2 cups BBQ sauce Hamburger buns Instructions Mix brown sugar and spices to create a dry rub Rub spice mixture all over the pork shoulder Place pork in slow cooker Cook on low for 8 hours until tender Shred meat with two forks Mix in BBQ sauce Serve on toasted buns

Classic Meatloaf

Classic Roast Chicken

Ingredients 1 whole chicken (4-5 lbs) 4 tbsp butter, softened 2 lemons, halved 1 head garlic, halved Fresh herbs (rosemary, thyme) Salt and pepper Instructions Preheat oven to 425°F Pat chicken dry with paper towels Rub butter under and over the skin Season generously with salt and pepper Stuff cavity with lemon halves, garlic, and herbs Tie legs together with kitchen twine Roast for 1.5 hours until golden and internal temp reaches 165°F Rest for 15 minutes before carving
