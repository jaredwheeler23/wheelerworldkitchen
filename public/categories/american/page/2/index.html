Creamy Mac and Cheese

Ingredients 1 lb elbow macaroni 4 tbsp butter 4 tbsp flour 3 cups milk 3 cups shredded cheddar cheese 1 cup shredded mozzarella Salt, pepper, and paprika 1 cup breadcrumbs 2 tbsp melted butter Instructions Cook macaroni according to package directions, drain In large pot, melt butter and whisk in flour Gradually add milk, whisking constantly Cook until thickened, about 5 minutes Remove from heat, stir in cheeses until melted Add cooked macaroni, season with salt, pepper, and paprika Transfer to greased baking dish Mix breadcrumbs with melted butter, sprinkle on top Bake at 375°F for 25-30 minutes until golden

Southern Fried Chicken

Ingredients 3 lbs chicken pieces 2 cups buttermilk 2 cups flour 2 tsp paprika 1 tsp garlic powder 1 tsp onion powder 1 tsp cayenne pepper Salt and pepper Vegetable oil for frying Instructions Marinate chicken in buttermilk for at least 2 hours Mix flour and all spices in large bowl Heat oil to 350°F in deep fryer or large pot Dredge chicken pieces in seasoned flour Fry in batches for 12-15 minutes until golden Drain on paper towels Season with additional salt while hot

Add lentils and wine and stir well, bring to lively simmer. Add 1.5 cups water and bring back to simmer. Lower heat, cover and cook until tender (about 40 minutes). Add more water if necessary. Taste at end and add salt if needed. Set aside. Mashed Potatoes Get a large/wide soup pot and fill one layer (only one layer) densely with potatoes. Then add water until just the last millimeter of potato is above the water line.
