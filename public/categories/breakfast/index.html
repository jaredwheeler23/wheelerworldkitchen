Apple Dutch Baby

Ingredients Main: 4 tbsp butter 2-3 apples, gala preferably 3-4 cup flour 3-4 cup whole milk, can be almond and doesn’t make difference 1/2 tsp salt 1 tbsp sugar 4 eggs Cinnamon Topping: ? cup sugar 2 tsp cinnamon Instructions Preheat oven to 400 F oven. Slice apples into 1/4 inch thick slices. Place butter into a 12" cast iron frying pan or dutch oven or whatever you go that can go in oven, and place in the oven to melt butter. In a bowl combine the flour, baking powder, sugar, salt, milk and eggs and whisk to combine. or place in a blender and pulse a few times to combine. Place the sliced apples into the bottom of the hot pan on top of the melted butter. Pour egg over apples. Work quickly so that the pan remains hot. Combine sugar and cinnamon and sprinkle over the top of the egg mixture evenly. Bake for 20-25 minutes until golden brown.

Breakfast Pizza

Ingredients 1 lb pork breakfast sausage 1 tsp black pepper 2 tbsp flour 2.25 cups milk 1 tsp apple cider vinegar 12 oz shredded mozzarella 2 tbsp dried sage Salt to taste 2 pounds pizza dough for one big pizza (see recipe) 1/4 cup sliced onion 4 eggs Any other optional toppings: jalapeno slices, diced bell pepper, etc. You can remove some of the meat and sub with veggies. Instructions Cook the sausage in a skillet until browned and fully cooked on med-high heat. Scoop out half of it for pizza toppings later and leave the remaining half in the pan. Stir in flour and black pepper and cook for 2 minutes stirring. Stir the milk into the pan. This should bubble up and thicken quick, ~2 minutes. Remove from heat once gravy is thickened. Add the vinegar and salt. Preheat oven to 500 F Prep a pizza tray with oil and/or corn starch so pizza doesn’t stick. Spread out dough making a nice pizza and place on tray. Add the gravy to pizza evenly. Layer mozzarella, sausage, sage, onion and rest of optional toppings. Crack eggs and plop them evenly spaced on top of the pizza. Bake in preheated oven for 8-10 minutes until crust is browned and cheese is melted. Eggs may look uncooked by appearance but they will be firm.
