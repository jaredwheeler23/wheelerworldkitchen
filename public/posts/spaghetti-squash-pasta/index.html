Spaghetti Squash Pasta

Simple roasted spaghetti squash - healthy pasta alternative

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 1 large Spaghetti Squash
  • Salt
  • Olive oil

Instructions

  1. Microwave the squash for 2 minutes (to make it easier to cut) and then cut in half lengthwise
  2. Drizzle on cut side of each half olive oil and salt
  3. Flip over onto a baking pan (with tinfoil) and bake for 45 minutes at 400°F
