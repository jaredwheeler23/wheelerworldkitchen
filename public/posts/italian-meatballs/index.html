Easy Italian Meatballs

Easy and tasty italian meatballs

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef (80/20), optional to do half and half beef and pork but beef alone is fine in a pinch
  • 1 large egg
  • 1/3 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1/3 cup grated parmesan cheese
  • 1/3 cup very finely chopped onion
  • 3 minced garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 3 tbsp finely chopped fresh parsley

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400 F and coat baking pan with oil.
  2. Combine all ingredients in a bowl and mix really well with hands.
  3. Use spoon or hands to make ~1.5 in diameter meatballs and space them on the oiled baking pan
  4. Bake for 18-20 minutes or until fully cooked through.
  5. Serve with whatever dish or by themselves. If serving by themselves, serve with marinara on the side.
