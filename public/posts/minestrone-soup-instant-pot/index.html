Minestrone Soup (Instant Pot)

Classic Italian vegetable soup made easy in the instant pot

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoon Olive Oil
  • 1 cup Onion diced
  • 5 cloves Garlic minced
  • 4 fresh roma tomatoes diced
  • 4 Celery Stalks chopped
  • 4 Carrots chopped
  • 2 small Zucchini chopped
  • 1/2 cup elbow macaroni pasta or similar
  • 32 ounces vegetable or chicken Broth
  • 1 can White Kidney Beans 15oz, drained
  • 2 cups Spinach chopped (can substitute kale but leave out the stems)
  • 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese grated or shredded
  • Parsley chopped, to garnish (optional)
  • 2 teaspoon Italian Seasoning, more to taste
  • 1 teaspoon Salt, to taste
  • 1/4 teaspoon Black Pepper crushed, to taste
  • 1 teaspoon Paprika, to taste

Instructions

  1. Start the instant pot in SAUTE mode with olive oil. Add diced onions and minced garlic. Stir and Saute for 2 minutes.
  2. Add diced tomatoes and chopped celery, carrots and zucchini. Add in the italian
  3. seasoning, salt, pepper and paprika. Stir another minute or so to mix well.
  4. Add in the pasta, vegetable broth and white kidney beans. Stir it all up. Make sure
  5. the pasta is under the broth.
  6. Press Cancel and close lid with vent in sealing position. Set to SOUP setting (high
  7. pressure) for 6 minutes. It will take a while to hit pressure and temp. 6 min starts AFTER that FYI.
  8. When 6 minutes are up and instant pot beeps, release the pressure.
  9. Stir in the chopped spinach and let it sit for 5 minutes. Stir in.
  10. Garnish with parmesan cheese (optional) and minestrone soup is ready to serve.
