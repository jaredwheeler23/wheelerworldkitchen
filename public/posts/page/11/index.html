Spicy Caribbean Fish

Sauce: 1 tbsp olive oil 1 large minced onion 1 tsp salt 1-2 hot pepper (hotter the better), minced 2 garlic cloves, minced 1 tsp thyme 2 cups diced fresh tomatoes ½ cup unsweetened coconut milk 1 tsp lime zest 2 tbsp lime juice 3 tbsp fresh cilantro Fish 2 pounds firm, white fish filets (any # to reach this weight is fine, big or small filets) Thaw prior to starting recipe or buy fresh filets. Black pepper ground and salt to taste Rice 1.5 cups raw rice plus 3 cups water, cook as per package directions and set aside Fresh Greens 2 bunches of kale (or 8 cups), cut into 1/2" or shorter sections 4-5 cloves garlic, minced Olive oil Few tbsps red pepper flakes, salt, black pepper Instructions Fish and sauce: Mince onion. Then heat oil and cook onion in sauce pan on medium heat for a few minutes. Mince pepper and garlic then add to sauce pan. Add thyme and cook for a few more minutes stirring often. Stir in the tomatoes, increase heat to med high, and cook for a couple minutes until tomatoes are juicy, stirring often. Add the coconut milk, bring to simmer, and cook a few minutes. Stir in the lime zest, juice, and cilantro. Stir and set sauce aside (if it cools, that is OK). Preheat oven to 400 F. Grease large baking pan with significant rim (to hold in the sauce), and place fish filets as single layer. Sprinkle with pepper and salt to taste and then bump ALL sauce into pan. Cook at temperature for 20-30 minutes. Serve over rice with extra sauce to soak in the rice. Instructions Fresh Greens: Heat oil and add garlic plus red pepper flakes. Saute for a minute or so. Then add kale as much as you can fit. Add more as it shrinks. Saute and add pepper/salt to taste until greens are limp and tender. Serve immediately. Notes: Greens have to be served immediately. Time it well or they will be cold. Extra sauce is not a bad thing. Can be served over coconut rice if you have it or quinoa.

Spicy Cauliflower Tomato Chili Gravy

Ingredients (sauce) Olive oil 1 large cauliflower cut into florets 1 med to large onion diced really well (or food processed into mush) 4-6 cloves garlic minced 2 inch ginger minced 2 large tomatoes diced 1-3 green chiles minced (more for more heat) 1 tbsp chili garlic sauce Spices 1.5 tsp cumin seeds 1 tsp turmeric 2.5 tsp ground coriander 1-3 tsp red chili powder (if you use 3 this is quite hot) 1 tsp garam masala 1 tsp crushed red pepper flakes Salt to taste Other 1 can coconut milk, full fat 0.5 cup water Tbsp lime Fresh cilantro (optional) Rice Instructions Heat oil on med high and add cumin seeds until starting to brown. Add onion, garlic, ginger, and pepper and saute until aromatic (1 min or less). Add all the spices and stir in for 20 seconds or so. Add tomatoes, cauliflower and chili garlic sauce. Stir well. Add coconut milk and water and simmer for 15 min while stirring occasionally. Add lime and cilantro and adjust salt to taste. Serve over rice.
