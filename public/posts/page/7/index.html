Shepherd's Pie

Ingredients 2 lbs ground lamb (or beef for Cottage Pie) 1 onion, diced 2 carrots, diced 1 cup peas 2 tbsp tomato paste 1 cup beef broth 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce 4 cups mashed potatoes 2 tbsp butter Salt and pepper Fresh thyme Instructions Brown ground lamb in large skillet, drain excess fat Add onion and carrots, cook until softened Stir in tomato paste, broth, Worcestershire, and thyme Add peas, simmer 10 minutes Transfer to baking dish Spread mashed potatoes evenly over top Dot with butter Bake at 400°F for 25-30 minutes until golden

Southern Fried Chicken

Ingredients 3 lbs chicken pieces 2 cups buttermilk 2 cups flour 2 tsp paprika 1 tsp garlic powder 1 tsp onion powder 1 tsp cayenne pepper Salt and pepper Vegetable oil for frying Instructions Marinate chicken in buttermilk for at least 2 hours Mix flour and all spices in large bowl Heat oil to 350°F in deep fryer or large pot Dredge chicken pieces in seasoned flour Fry in batches for 12-15 minutes until golden Drain on paper towels Season with additional salt while hot

Spaghetti Carbonara

Ingredients 1 lb spaghetti 6 oz pancetta or bacon, diced 4 eggs 1 cup Parmesan cheese, grated Black pepper Salt Instructions Cook spaghetti in salted boiling water until al dente While pasta cooks, fry pancetta until crispy Beat eggs with Parmesan and lots of black pepper Reserve 1 cup pasta water, then drain pasta Remove pan from heat, add hot pasta to pancetta Quickly stir in egg mixture, adding pasta water to create creamy sauce Serve immediately with extra Parmesan and pepper
