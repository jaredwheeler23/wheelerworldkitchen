Chicken Teriyaki

Ingredients 2 lbs chicken thighs 1/2 cup soy sauce 1/4 cup mirin 1/4 cup sake or white wine 3 tbsp sugar 2 cloves garlic, minced 1 tbsp ginger, minced 1 tbsp cornstarch Sesame seeds Green onions Instructions Mix soy sauce, mirin, sake, sugar, garlic, and ginger Marinate chicken for 15 minutes Remove chicken, reserve marinade Grill or pan-fry chicken until cooked through Simmer reserved marinade with cornstarch until thickened Brush glaze over cooked chicken Garnish with sesame seeds and green onions Serve over rice

Chile Verde

Ingredients 3 lbs pork shoulder, cubed 2 lbs tomatillos, husked and halved 2 poblano peppers, roasted and peeled 2 jalapeños 1 onion, quartered 4 cloves garlic 1 cup chicken broth 1 tsp cumin Fresh cilantro Salt and pepper Instructions Roast tomatillos and peppers under broiler until charred Blend roasted vegetables with onion, garlic, and broth Brown pork in batches in Dutch oven Add blended sauce and cumin Simmer covered for 4 hours until pork is tender Season with salt and pepper Garnish with fresh cilantro Serve with rice or in tortillas

Chocolate Chip Brownies

Ingredients 1 cup butter, melted 2 cups sugar 4 eggs 1 1/2 tsp vanilla 2/3 cup cocoa powder 1 cup flour 1/2 tsp salt 1/2 tsp baking powder 1 1/2 cups chocolate chips Instructions Preheat oven to 350°F, grease 9x13 pan Mix melted butter and sugar Beat in eggs and vanilla Combine cocoa, flour, salt, and baking powder Stir dry ingredients into wet ingredients Fold in chocolate chips Pour into prepared pan Bake 25-30 minutes (don’t overbake for fudgy brownies) Cool completely before cutting

Chocolate Lava Cakes

Ingredients 6 oz dark chocolate, chopped 1/2 cup butter 2 eggs 2 egg yolks 1/4 cup sugar 2 tbsp flour Pinch of salt Butter and cocoa for ramekins Vanilla ice cream for serving Instructions Butter and cocoa dust 4 ramekins Melt chocolate and butter together Whisk eggs, egg yolks, and sugar until thick Fold in melted chocolate Gently fold in flour and salt Divide among ramekins Bake at 425°F for 12-14 minutes Centers should be soft Let stand 1 minute, then invert onto plates Serve immediately with vanilla ice cream

Classic Beef Tacos

Ingredients 2 lbs ground beef 1 onion, diced 3 cloves garlic, minced 2 tbsp chili powder 1 tsp cumin 1 tsp paprika Salt and pepper 12 taco shells Toppings: lettuce, tomatoes, cheese, sour cream, salsa Instructions Brown ground beef in large skillet over medium-high heat Add onion and garlic, cook until softened Drain excess fat Add spices and 1/2 cup water Simmer for 10 minutes until mixture thickens Warm taco shells according to package directions Fill shells with beef and desired toppings

Classic Coleslaw

Ingredients 1 head green cabbage, shredded 2 carrots, grated 1 cup mayonnaise 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar 1 tbsp sugar 1 tsp celery seed Salt and pepper Instructions Combine shredded cabbage and carrots in large bowl In separate bowl, whisk together mayo, vinegar, sugar, and celery seed Pour dressing over cabbage mixture Toss until well coated Season with salt and pepper Refrigerate at least 1 hour before serving

Classic Meatloaf

Ingredients 2 lbs ground beef 1 cup breadcrumbs 1 onion, finely chopped 2 eggs, beaten 1/2 cup milk 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce Salt and pepper For glaze: 1/2 cup ketchup 2 tbsp brown sugar 1 tbsp mustard Instructions Preheat oven to 350°F Mix all meatloaf ingredients in large bowl Shape into loaf and place in baking dish Mix glaze ingredients and brush over meatloaf Bake for 1 hour until internal temp reaches 160°F Let rest 10 minutes before slicing

Classic Roast Chicken

Ingredients 1 whole chicken (4-5 lbs) 4 tbsp butter, softened 2 lemons, halved 1 head garlic, halved Fresh herbs (rosemary, thyme) Salt and pepper Instructions Preheat oven to 425°F Pat chicken dry with paper towels Rub butter under and over the skin Season generously with salt and pepper Stuff cavity with lemon halves, garlic, and herbs Tie legs together with kitchen twine Roast for 1.5 hours until golden and internal temp reaches 165°F Rest for 15 minutes before carving
