Classic Tiramisu

Ingredients 6 egg yolks 3/4 cup sugar 1 1/3 cups mascarpone cheese 2 cups heavy cream 2 cups strong espresso, cooled 3 tbsp coffee liqueur (optional) 40 ladyfinger cookies Cocoa powder for dusting Dark chocolate shavings (optional) Instructions Whisk egg yolks and sugar until thick and pale Add mascarpone, beat until smooth Whip heavy cream to stiff peaks, fold into mascarpone mixture Mix espresso with coffee liqueur Quickly dip ladyfingers in espresso mixture Layer dipped ladyfingers in 9x13 dish Spread half the mascarpone cream over ladyfingers Repeat with another layer Dust generously with cocoa powder Refrigerate at least 4 hours or overnight

Coq au Vin

Ingredients 3 lbs chicken pieces 6 oz bacon, diced 8 oz mushrooms, quartered 12 pearl onions, peeled 3 cloves garlic, minced 2 carrots, sliced 2 cups red wine 1 cup chicken broth 2 tbsp tomato paste 3 tbsp flour 3 tbsp butter Fresh thyme and bay leaf Salt and pepper Instructions Brown bacon in Dutch oven, set aside Season chicken, brown in bacon fat, set aside Sauté mushrooms and onions, set aside Add carrots and garlic to pot, cook 2 minutes Stir in flour, then add wine, broth, and tomato paste Add thyme and bay leaf Return chicken and bacon to pot Simmer covered for 1.5 hours Add mushrooms and onions, cook 15 more minutes Serve with crusty bread or mashed potatoes

Creamy Mac and Cheese

Crème Brûlée

Ingredients 2 cups heavy cream 1 vanilla bean (or 2 tsp vanilla extract) 5 egg yolks 1/2 cup sugar (plus extra for topping) Pinch of salt Instructions Preheat oven to 325°F Heat cream with vanilla bean (split and scraped) until simmering Whisk egg yolks with sugar and salt Slowly pour hot cream into egg mixture, whisking constantly Strain through fine mesh sieve Pour into 6 ramekins Place ramekins in roasting pan, add hot water halfway up sides Bake 40-45 minutes until set but still jiggly Refrigerate at least 3 hours Before serving, sprinkle sugar on top and caramelize with torch
