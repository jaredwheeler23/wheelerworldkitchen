Mushroom Risotto

Ingredients 1.5 cups Arborio rice 8 oz mushrooms, sliced 6 cups chicken or vegetable broth, warmed 1 onion, finely diced 3 cloves garlic, minced 1/2 cup white wine 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese 3 tbsp butter 2 tbsp olive oil Fresh parsley Salt and pepper Instructions Sauté mushrooms in butter until golden, set aside In same pot, heat olive oil and cook onion until soft Add garlic and rice, stir for 2 minutes Add wine and stir until absorbed Add broth one ladle at a time, stirring constantly Continue adding broth until rice is creamy and al dente (about 25 minutes) Stir in mushrooms, Parmesan, and remaining butter Season with salt and pepper, garnish with parsley

New York Cheesecake

Ingredients Crust: 2 cups graham cracker crumbs 1/2 cup melted butter 1/4 cup sugar Filling: 32 oz cream cheese, softened 1 1/4 cups sugar 4 eggs 1 cup sour cream 2 tsp vanilla extract 1/4 cup flour Instructions Mix graham cracker crumbs, melted butter, and sugar Press into bottom of 9-inch springform pan Bake crust at 325°F for 10 minutes Beat cream cheese until fluffy Add sugar, beat until smooth Add eggs one at a time Mix in sour cream, vanilla, and flour Pour over crust Bake at 325°F for 55-60 minutes (center should jiggle slightly) Turn off oven, leave cheesecake inside for 1 hour Refrigerate at least 4 hours before serving

Osso Buco

Ingredients 6 veal shanks Flour for dredging 2 carrots, diced 2 celery stalks, diced 1 onion, diced 4 cloves garlic, minced 1 cup white wine 2 cups beef broth 28 oz can crushed tomatoes 2 bay leaves Fresh thyme Salt and pepper Gremolata: lemon zest, parsley, garlic Instructions Season veal shanks, dredge in flour Brown shanks in olive oil, set aside Sauté carrots, celery, and onion until soft Add garlic, cook 1 minute Add wine, simmer until reduced Add broth, tomatoes, bay leaves, and thyme Return shanks to pot, bring to simmer Cover and braise in 325°F oven for 2.5 hours Top with gremolata before serving Serve with risotto

Pad Thai

Ingredients 8 oz rice noodles 1 lb shrimp, peeled 3 eggs 3 tbsp tamarind paste 2 tbsp fish sauce 2 tbsp sugar 3 cloves garlic, minced Bean sprouts Green onions, chopped Peanuts, crushed Lime wedges Vegetable oil Instructions Soak rice noodles in warm water for 30 minutes, drain Mix tamarind paste, fish sauce, and sugar for sauce Heat oil in wok, scramble eggs and set aside Stir-fry shrimp until pink, set aside Add garlic to wok, then add noodles Pour sauce over noodles, toss to coat Add shrimp and eggs back to wok Top with bean sprouts, green onions, and peanuts Serve with lime wedges
