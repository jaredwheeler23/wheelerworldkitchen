Butterscotch Pudding with Chocolate Scribbles

A decadent homemade butterscotch pudding with scotch whiskey, topped with chocolate decorations.

Ingredients

Pudding

  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 3 tbsp cornstarch
  • 7/8 cup dark brown sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1/8 cup scotch whiskey (or any whiskey works well too)

Chocolate Scribbles

  • 6 ounces bittersweet chocolate finely chopped

Instructions

  1. In medium bowl, whisk 1/2 cup of milk with the cornstarch, sugar, eggs and salt.

  2. In large saucepan, cook remaining amount of milk with the heavy cream over medium heat until a skin forms on the surface (a few minutes).

  3. Slowly whisk both together in the saucepan, reduce to medium low heat, and whisk fast/constantly until the pudding thickens and just begins to boil (about 5 minutes or more). Remove from heat and whisk in the butter and scotch.

  4. Spoon the pudding into 6 ounce glasses or jars and refrigerate until set, minimum of 4 hours (preferably overnight). See notes.

  5. Meanwhile, to make the scribbles: line a baking sheet with parchment paper (or equivalent). Heat the chocolate in a bowl in the microwave until mostly melted. Stir to mix all together until smooth. Place chocolate in a small Ziploc bag and cut a very small amount from the bottom corner. Squeeze chocolate out onto baking sheet into fun shapes (best to keep each bite 1x2 inches in size, for best dipping). Let set in the fridge for 30 minutes or more.

  6. Serve the pudding with 1 or 2 scribbles dipped in the top. One short burst of whip cream on top is nice.

Notes

  • Do not short change the pudding mixture set time. This pudding can keep for days so don’t be afraid to make in the morning or the day before.
  • Whisking well is very important. It is super easy for the pudding to come out lumpy if not enough stirring.
