Spicy Italian Sausage Korean Stir Fry

Fusion dish combining Italian sausage with Korean-style spicy stir fry sauce

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • Olive oil for cooking (just a few tbsp at most)
  • 1 lb spicy italian sausages (NOT ground, the sausages)
  • 0.33 cup soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce
  • 3 tbsp white sugar
  • 1 small yellow onion diced
  • 1 green bell pepper diced
  • 1 large head of broccoli, just the crowns chopped into small crown sizes
  • 1/2 cup chili garlic sauce
  • 1 cup rice cooked to package directions

Instructions

  1. Grill the sausages whole until cooked through. Then chop into 0.5 in bits. Have ready to go
  2. Combine soy sauce, fish and sugar in small bowl
  3. In large nonstick pan over medium high heat, add some oil and cook the veggies until just tender
  4. Add sausage and chili garlic sauce and cook for another minute or two stirring well
  5. Add soy sauce mixture and continue cooking over med heat until things are fully cooked and mixed. Cook another few minutes. Add some water if it starts to dry out
  6. Serve over rice
