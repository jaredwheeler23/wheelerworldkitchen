Autumn Pot Pie (Christmas Veggie Pot Pie)

A stunning vegetarian pot pie perfect for the holidays, featuring butternut squash and a homemade cheddar crust.

Ingredients

Cheddar Pastry

  • 2 cups all purpose white flour
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup cold butter, diced
  • 1 cup chilled, grated sharp cheddar cheese (buy a bag of shredded cheese)
  • 4-5 tbsp ice water

Vegetable Filling

  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 4 cups chopped leeks (use only white and light green parts of 2 medium leeks)
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 tsp dried sage (or 1 tbsp fresh)
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 cups green beans (cut into 1 in. lengths)
  • 3/4 cup vegetable stock
  • 3 cups chopped green cabbage
  • 3 cups cubed butternut squash
  • 3 cups sliced mushrooms

Cheese Sauce

  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 1/4 cup all purpose white flour
  • 1.5 cups warmed vegetable stock
  • 1/2 tsp dijon mustard or similar
  • 1/2 cup grated cheese, parmesan works
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Start with cheddar pastry. Use a food processor and briefly pulse the flour, salt, and butter until the mixture has lumps about the size of peas. Add cheddar and pulse a few times until the mixture resembles a coarse meal. Add the water, a tbsp at a time, pulsing briefly to incorporate. Press the dough into a ball. Wrap in waxed paper or plastic wrap and refrigerate until ready to roll out.

  2. Make the filling. In a large saucepan or pot, warm oil on medium high and add leeks, bay leaves, thyme, sage and salt. Saute for about 3 minutes. Add green beans and the stock to simmer for 5 minutes. Add cabbage and squash and cook for another 8-10 minutes until the veggies are becoming tender. Add mushrooms and cook for another 3 minutes. Remove from heat and cover. The goal is to have barely tender veggies that will hold their shape in the baked pot pie.

  3. Make the cheese sauce. In a small saucepan, melt the butter and whisk in the flour, whisking for a couple minutes to “cook” the flour and then continue whisking while slowly adding the vegetable stock and it thickens into a sauce. Add mustard and cheese and whisk until smooth. Stir the sauce into the vegetable filling and season with salt and pepper to taste. Pour this into a 9x13 in. baking dish.

  4. Preheat the oven to 375 F.

  5. On a lightly floured surface, roll out the dough to fit over the baking dish. Cut a few slits in the crust to allow steam to vent for a crisper crust. Place on top of the filling and trim the edges.

  6. Bake for 40-50 minutes until the crust is golden. Let sit for 10 minutes prior to serving.
