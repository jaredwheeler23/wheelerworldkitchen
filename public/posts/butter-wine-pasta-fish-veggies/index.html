Butter Wine Pasta with White Fish Filets & Veggies

Light pasta dish with baked fish, vegetables, and white wine butter sauce

Ingredients

Ingredients

Fish:

  • 5 white fish (cod or anything) filets thawed
  • Oil
  • All seasonings below sprinkled on top
  • Touch of lime juice on all

Pasta Stuff:

  • 0.6 lb pasta, preferably short pasta (cook according to package directions)

  • 3 cups of veggies, dealer’s choice (ex: zucchini, onion, asparagus diced small, etc.)

  • 0.5 cup parmesan cheese (the hard, good kind)

  • 2 tbsp butter

  • ? cup wine

  • Few tbsp olive oil

  • 4 oz can green chilis

  • 5 cloves garlic minced

  • Spices: black pepper, garlic salt, cayenne, touch sriracha, italian seasonings

On the side at the table

  • More parmesan if you want it.

Instructions

  1. Thaw fish, season and bake at 400 for 15 minutes or until easily flaked. Do not overcook.
  2. Pan fry veggies, garlic, green chilis with olive oil and butter until mostly soft. Add seasonings, dealer choice on quantity. Add wine, parmesan, cover and cook for 5-10 minutes. Add cooked pasta, stir well, cover and turn off heat.
  3. Serve pasta in a pasta bowl and top with a filet or two. Add additional parmesan as desired.
