Creamy Corn Street Tacos

Vegetarian street tacos with creamy elote-style corn and mushrooms

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 3 cups of corn (from corn on cob (in that case boil and cook and cut off) or from frozen or can

  • 1/4 cup mayo

  • 2 tbsp sour cream

  • 1/2 cup crumbled cotija cheese (or fresca if that is all that is available)

  • 2 tbsp lime juice

  • 1/2 tsp salt

  • 2 tsp everything but the elote seasoning

  • 2 tbsp olive oil

  • 3 large cloves garlic minced

  • Package of sliced portobello mushrooms

  • Package of street tortillas, corn or flour

  • 1 large avocado sliced

  • Some cilantro diced tomato (that was seeded and cored out)

Instructions

  1. Mix first set together. It helps if corn is [piping hot when mixed as that will bring it all to a good temp. Or microwave it carefully after…
  2. Stir fry the mushrooms and garlic until cooked through.
  3. Assemble: tortilla - > some corn mixture warm -> some mushroom -> cilantro/avocado/tomato/sour cream/salsa/everything but elote seasoning as desired. (typically a quantity of 4 tacos is a meal per person)
